# Fifteen-Day Ascent to Presence: Do All the Days

*Psalms 120-134 (Songs of Ascent) + Isaiah anchors. Read to become visitable. 20-30 min per day: Read, Anchor, Hebrew, Do, Clay prayer, Write.*

> "They that trust in the Lord shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever." (Ps. 125:1)
> "Seek ye the Lord while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near." (Isa. 55:6)

Read both passages aloud each day. Pray the clay line with your specifics and write one sentence of what He taught. Memorize the verse.

---

## Days 1-3: Leave Babylon glad

Leave Babylon by naming it: honest confession comes before kept help and glad ascent.

### Day 1: Honest in Meshech (Ps 120 + Isa 1:16-18, 6:5, 59:3)
Read Ps 120. "Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech... My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace. I am for peace: but when I speak, they are for war" (120:5-7). Name distance, lying lips (120:2-3).
Anchor: "Wash you, make you clean... Come now, and let us reason together... though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow" (Isa 1:16-18). "Woe is me! for I am undone... unclean lips" (Isa 6:5). "Your lips have spoken lies" (Isa 59:3).
Hebrew: *oy-li* woe-is-me (6:5, distinct from six *hoy* judgments in Isa 5) owns guilt before grace lifts. *rachatsu* wash (Qal, simple action) + *hizzakku* make-yourselves-clean (Hitpael, reflexive): you step in.
DO: write one deception/harm to stop + one good to learn (Isa 1:17 relieve, judge fatherless). Reason aloud with Him 5 min.
Clay: "Father, I am in Meshech with lying lips. Wash me, make snow."
Write: what scarlet did I reason about?
Memorize Isa 1:18.

### Day 2: Keeper (Ps 121 + Isa 40:28-31, 2:2, 4:5-6)
Read Ps 121. "I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills... My help cometh from the Lord... He will not suffer thy foot to be moved... He that keepeth thee will not slumber" (121:1-4). Keeper (*shomer*, guard) 6x.
Anchor: "Hast thou not known... the everlasting God... fainteth not... they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles" (Isa 40:28-31). "Mountain of the Lord's house shall be established in the top of the mountains" (Isa 2:2). "There shall be a tabernacle for a shadow... covert" (Isa 4:6).
Hebrew: *shamar* keeper-guard, *tsel* shade-cover, *qavah* braid-wait, *alah* up.
DO: list what you self-keep. Transfer one to Him. 3x look up and whisper "My help."
Clay: "Keeper, I stop keeping myself. Shadow me, renew wings."
Write: what foot did He keep?
Memorize Ps 121:1-2.

### Day 3: Glad to go up (Ps 122 + Isa 2:3-5, 11:9, 62:6-7)
Read Ps 122. "I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord. Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem... Pray for the peace of Jerusalem" (122:1-2,6).
Anchor: "Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord... he will teach us of his ways... O house of Jacob, come ye, and let us walk in the light" (Isa 2:3,5). "They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain" (Isa 11:9).
Hebrew: *alah* go up, *shalom/shalem* peace-wholeness wordplay, *yachdav* knit-compact.
DO: pray for peace of ward/city/Israel by name. Send one peacemaking text. Plan next temple time with written question.
Clay: "Father, set my feet in gates, make me peacemaker."
Write: when was I glad to go up?
Memorize Ps 122:1.

## Days 4-6: Hold (low gaze to stability)

Hold steady with eyes low: the servant upward gaze is what keeps the foot from slipping.

### Day 4: Servant eyes (Ps 123 + Isa 30:18-19, 8:17, 45:22)
Read Ps 123. "Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens. As eyes of servants... so our eyes wait upon the Lord... until he have mercy" (123:1-2).
Anchor: "Therefore will the Lord wait, that he may be gracious... blessed are all they that wait for him... he will be very gracious... at voice of thy cry" (Isa 30:18-19). "I will wait upon the Lord... and I will look for him" (Isa 8:17). "Look unto me, and be ye saved" (Isa 45:22).
Hebrew: *avadim/shiphchah* servants, *enayim el-yad* eyes to hand, *chanan* stoop-mercy, *qavah* cord-wait.
DO: 3x today 60-sec lift eyes, silence before petition.
Clay: "Enthroned, eyes on Thy hand, mercy me, shape wanting."
Write: where did eyes dart first?
Memorize Ps 123:2.

### Day 5: Escaped flood (Ps 124 + Isa 43:1-3, 8:7-8, 59:19)
Read Ps 124. "If it had not been the Lord... then the waters had overwhelmed us... Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare... Our help is in the name of the Lord" (124:1,4,7-8).
Anchor: "Fear not: for I have redeemed thee... When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee" (Isa 43:1-2). "When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit... shall lift up a standard" (Isa 59:19).
Hebrew: *lule YHWH* if not, *mayim/nachal/zedonim bala nephesh* proud waters swallow soul, *tsippor* bird escape, *beshem YHWH* in Name.
DO: write 4-line If-not-Lord testimony, share.
Clay: "On-my-side Redeemer, break snare, teach soul to fly."
Write: what would have swallowed me?
Memorize Ps 124:8.

### Day 6: Encircled Zion (Ps 125 + Isa 28:16, 26:3-4, 46:4)
Read Ps 125. "They that trust... shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed... As mountains round Jerusalem, so the Lord round his people" (125:1-2).
Anchor: "Behold, I lay in Zion... a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste" (Isa 28:16). "Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed... for in the Lord JEHOVAH is everlasting strength" (Isa 26:3-4). "Even to hoar hairs will I carry you" (Isa 46:4).
Hebrew: *habbotchim kehar Tsiyyon lo-yimmot* shall not totter, *sabib* surround, *samakh* uphold.
DO: pick one anxious decision, not-hasten 24h, read 28:16 morn/eve.
Clay: "Cornerstone, surround me, make unmovable."
Write: where do I hasten?
Memorize Ps 125:1.

## Days 7-9: Home (joy to table)

Come home to joy: the Lord turns captivity to song, builds the house, and spreads the table.

### Day 7: Dreamers sow (Ps 126 + Isa 35:10, 51:11, 65:18)
Read Ps 126. "When the Lord turned again captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream... They that sow in tears shall reap in joy" (126:1,5).
Anchor: "And the ransomed... shall return, and come to Zion with songs and everlasting joy... sorrow and sighing shall flee away" (Isa 35:10). "I create Jerusalem a rejoicing, and her people a joy" (Isa 65:18).
Hebrew: *hayinu kecholmim* stunned healing, *rinnah* ringing shout, *zera* seed: tears irrigation.
DO: write one great-things memory, sing/share aloud.
Clay: "Turn dreaming memory to sowing faith."
Write: what tear will I sow?
Memorize Ps 126:5.

### Day 8: House He builds (Ps 127 + Isa 44:26,28, 54:2-3, 65:22-23)
Read Ps 127. "Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain... children are an heritage... as arrows" (127:1,3-4).
Anchor: "That saith to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited... to the temple, Thy foundation shall be laid" (Isa 44:26,28). "Enlarge... lengthen... strengthen thy stakes... thy seed shall inherit" (Isa 54:2-3). "They shall not labour in vain... they are the seed of the blessed" (Isa 65:22-23).
Hebrew: *banah* build vs *shav* vapor, *hinneh nachalat banim* children are building.
DO: no-phone prayer before work, dedicate home work, sleep as trust (127:2).
Clay: "Build through me, lest vain."
Write: what anxious building do I yield?
Memorize Ps 127:1.

### Day 9: Table blessed (Ps 128 + Isa 65:23, 61:9, 54:13)
Read Ps 128. "Blessed every one that feareth... labour of hands eaten... wife as fruitful vine... children as olive plants... blessing out of Zion" (128:1-5).
Anchor: "They are the seed of the blessed... and their offspring with them" (Isa 65:23). "All thy children shall be taught of the Lord; and great shall be the peace of thy children" (Isa 54:13).
Hebrew: *ashrei yare* flourishing fearer, *yegia kappeikha* toil redeemed, vine/olives domestic Eden, *shalom al-Yisrael*.
DO: one unhurried meal, bless spouse/child, pray Zion from table.
Clay: "Make house vineyard, peace Israel."
Write: is home waystation or vineyard?
Memorize Ps 128:1.

---

## Days 10-12: Veil center

Pass through the center of the veil: the plowed back, the depths, and the weaned child lead to forgiveness and stillness.

### Day 10: Plowed enduring (Ps 129 + Isa 50:6-7, 53:5, 30:15)
Read Ps 129. "Many a time afflicted me from youth... plowers plowed upon my back... The Lord is righteous: he hath cut asunder the cords" (129:1-4).
Anchor: "I gave my back to the smiters... I hid not face... I set my face like a flint" (Isa 50:6-7). "With his stripes we are healed" (Isa 53:5). "In quietness and in confidence shall be your strength" (Isa 30:15).
Hebrew: *charash al-gabbi* plow back-field, *qitsets avot* cut cords, *Tsaddiq* severs.
DO: 24h no-retaliation, one steady obedience, write cord to cut.
Clay: "Cut cords, set face, make scars witness."
Write: what furrow do I rehearse?
Memorize Ps 129:4.

### Day 11: Depths (Ps 130 + Isa 53:5-6, 43:25, 44:22, 55:7)
Read Ps 130. "Out of the depths... If thou shouldest mark iniquities, who shall stand? But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared... wait... redeem" (130:1-4,7-8).
Anchor: "He was wounded for our transgressions... bruised... chastisement of our peace... with his stripes healed. All we like sheep... Lord hath laid on him iniquity" (Isa 53:5-6). "I... am he that blotteth out... for mine own sake" (Isa 43:25). "Return... for I have redeemed thee" (Isa 44:22). "Let him return... he will abundantly pardon" (Isa 55:7).
Hebrew: *mimma'amaqim* depths, *selichah* forgiveness→reverence, *mecholal/medukka* pierced/crushed, *nirpa-lanu* healing to us.
DO: name one sin, confess forsake, 10-min watcher silence, record mercy.
Clay: "Out of depths cry, blot cloud, teach wait."
Write: what ledger do I keep that He blots?
Memorize Ps 130:4.

### Day 12: Weaned child (Ps 131 + Isa 57:15, 6:5-7, 29:19)
Read Ps 131. "My heart is not haughty, nor eyes lofty, neither great matters... I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child weaned... hope" (131:1-3).
Anchor: "I dwell in high holy place, with him also that is of contrite and humble spirit, to revive" (Isa 57:15). "Woe is me... Lo, this hath touched... iniquity taken away" (Isa 6:5,7). "The meek shall increase joy" (Isa 29:19).
Hebrew: *gamul* weaned resting, *shivviti ve-domamti* composed stilled, *daka/shefal* crushed low.
DO: one unseen service, surrender one great matter, 5-min stillness.
Clay: "Still me gamul, high with Thee by low."
Write: where haughty/lofty/grasping?
Memorize Ps 131:2.

---

## Days 13-15: Dwell and bless

Dwell and bless: settle into His rest, receive the oil, and bless by night until Zion shines.

### Day 13: Ark rest (Ps 132 + Isa 11:10, 55:3, 52:1, 66:1-2)
Read Ps 132. "Arise, O Lord, into thy rest... For the Lord hath chosen Zion... This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell" (132:8,13-14).
Anchor: "There shall be a root of Jesse... his rest shall be glorious" (Isa 11:10). "Incline ear... hear, soul live; I will make everlasting covenant... sure mercies of David" (Isa 55:3). "Awake... put on beautiful garments" (Isa 52:1). "To this man will I look... poor contrite trembleth at word" (Isa 66:1-2).
Hebrew: *menuchah* settled rest, *qum* ark cry, *shakan* dwell, *avah* desire.
DO: make ark place same hour daily no phone 10 min.
Clay: "Arise into me, settle glory small Zion."
Write: do I visit or house Him?
Memorize Ps 132:14.

### Day 14: Oil (Ps 133 + Isa 61:1, 26:19, 58:8-11)
Read Ps 133. "Behold, how good... brethren dwell together in unity! As precious ointment upon head... as dew of Hermon... there commanded blessing, life for evermore" (133:1-3).
Anchor: "The Spirit... hath anointed me to preach... bind up brokenhearted, proclaim liberty" (Isa 61:1). "Thy dew is as dew of herbs... earth shall cast out dead" (Isa 26:19). "Then shall thy light break forth... thou shalt be like watered garden" (Isa 58:8,11).
Hebrew: *mashach/shemen* anoint, *yachad* one, *tal* silent grace, *yarad* descends.
DO: one anointing act apologize/comfort/testify.
Clay: "Pour oil head to hem, dew to dry."
Write: where oil blocked (Isa 59:2)?
Memorize Ps 133:3.

### Day 15: Night blessers (Ps 134 + Isa 62:6-7, 60:1, 52:7-8)
Read Ps 134. "Behold, bless ye the Lord, all servants which by night stand in house... Lift up hands... The Lord... bless thee out of Zion" (134:1-3).
Anchor: "I have set watchmen... which shall never hold peace day nor night... give him no rest, till he establish... Jerusalem a praise" (Isa 62:6-7). "Arise, shine; for thy light is come" (Isa 60:1). "How beautiful... feet... Thy watchmen... shall see eye to eye" (Isa 52:7-8).
Hebrew: *barak* kneel-bless reciprocal, *lailah/amad* night watch, *hinneh* behold! call (hineni exchange is Isa 58:9 / 6:8).
DO: one night watch prayer, lift hands literally, bless sleeping child, temple names.
Clay: "In night stand, fire clay dark kiln, bless till Zion."
Write: do I bless only when lit?
Memorize Ps 134:2.

---

Finish with Ps 24 aloud: Who shall ascend? Clean hands pure heart. Lift heads, King of glory in. Answer Isa 6:8 Here am I send me + Isa 58:9 Here I am to you.
