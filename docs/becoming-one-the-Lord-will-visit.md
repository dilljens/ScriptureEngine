# Becoming One the Lord Will Visit: An Isaiah Manual for Transformation

*For personal study. Isaiah teaches how to become one the Lord will visit: wash, ascend, confess, hear, be carried, awake, and feast. Seeing Him is real and conditional on sanctification. Built from Isaiah 1-66 read through Nephi's keys (2 Nephi 11; 25:1-8), Christ's command (3 Nephi 23:1-3), and the D&C (84, 88:68, 93:1, 97, 113).*

> "And now I write some of the words of Isaiah, that whoso of my people who shall see these words may lift up their hearts and rejoice for all men." — 2 Nephi 11:8
> "Sanctify yourselves that your minds become single to God, and the days will come that you shall see him; for he will unveil his face unto you." — D&C 88:68
> "Every soul who forsaketh his sins and cometh unto me, and calleth on my name, and obeyeth my voice, and keepeth my commandments, shall see my face, and know that I am." — D&C 93:1

## 1. The promise is real, and conditional

Scripture does not treat seeing the Lord as metaphor only, nor as reserved for prophets:

- Moses saw God face to face (Moses 1:2, 11; Ex. 33:11)
- Isaiah saw the Lord high and lifted up (Isa. 6:1)
- Nephi and Jacob saw Him (2 Ne. 11:2-3)
- The brother of Jared could not be kept within the veil (Ether 3:13, 20)
- The Nephite disciples were purified to behold Him (3 Ne. 19:28-29)
- He promises you: forsake, come, call, obey, keep: see (D&C 93:1)

Isaiah's whole book is how an unclean people becomes a holy people who can abide that weight of glory without being consumed by it. Hebrews 12:14: without holiness no man shall see the Lord. Psalm 24:3-4: clean hands, pure heart shall ascend.

A visit does not always mean the same form: dream, voice, angel, veil thin in temple, Second Comforter, final coming. Isaiah covers all of them. Your work is the same: become visitable.

## 2. What Hebrew shows that English hides

English hides what Hebrew shows: holiness is separateness for use, cleanness is fitness to enter, glory is weight to bear, presence is face to seek, and repentance is return home.

### Holiness is separateness for use, not just goodness
Root q-d-sh (holy, set apart): qadosh (holy), qiddesh (Piel, to make holy), hitqaddesh (Hitpael, sanctify yourself; cf. Josh. 3:5), miqdash (sanctuary, temple).
"Holy, holy, holy" (Isa. 6:3, qadosh, qadosh, qadosh) is Hebrew superlative: completely, utterly set apart. Threefold = Father, Son, Holy Ghost witness, heaven-earth-fullness.
To be sanctified is to be taken out of common use (Babylon) and set apart for God's use (Zion). Hence Isa. 52:11: depart, touch no unclean, be clean: separation precedes presence.

Agency matters: Isa. 1:16 "rachatsu (Qal active, wash!) hizzakku (Hitpael, make-yourselves-clean)." The second verb is reflexive. You participate. God provides water and blood, but you step in. Same with Josh. 3:5 hitqaddeshu and D&C 88:68 "sanctify yourselves." Grace does not bypass agency.

### Clean is tahor: ritually fit to enter
tahor / tihar: fit to come near. Isa. 35:8: the highway shall be called Way of Holiness, the unclean (tame) shall not pass. Isa. 52:11: be clean (hibbaru, Niphal of barar, purify/select yourselves) ye that bear vessels. Note: Ps. 24:3-4 gate uses different words, neqi khappayim (innocent of hands) and uvar-levav (pure of heart), which mean innocence and purity, not cultic tahor language.
Psalm 51:10 "lev tahor bera-li": create in me a clean heart. Only God can create it, but you must bring the broken heart (Ps. 51:17; 3 Ne. 9:20; Isa. 66:2).

### Glory is weight: kavod
kavod from kaved (heavy). God's presence has mass. Isaiah's temple fills with train and smoke (Isa. 6:1-4). The unclean cannot bear it (D&C 76:116-118; 84:23 they could not endure presence). Sanctification is gaining capacity to bear weight, as a bridge is strengthened to bear load.

### Face is presence: panim
panim means face, front, presence. To seek face (biqqesh panim, Ps. 27:8; Isa. 55:6 dirshu) is to seek audience, not information. Hester panim, the hiding of face (Isa. 8:17; 59:2 your iniquities have separated, hidden His face), is exile. Panim el panim, face to face, is return from exile.
Isa. 52:6: "my people shall know (yada) my name... that it is I." Yada is covenant knowing by experience, as Adam knew Eve. You do not learn about Him; you know Him.

### Return, not just remorse: shuv
Repentance in Isaiah is shuv: turn, return. Isa. 55:7: let the wicked forsake his way, unrighteous his thoughts, and return (veyashov) unto the Lord, and He will abundantly pardon. Isa. 30:15: in returning (shuvah) and rest shall ye be saved. You are not groveling; you are coming home.

### Atonement is covering: kaphar
Isa. 6:7: "vesar avonekha vechattatekha tekhuppar": thy sin shall be atoned for (Pual of kaphar), by coal from altar touching lips. Same root as Yom Kippur. Speech is where impurity shows (unclean lips, 6:5; oy-li, woe-is-me, distinct from the six hoy judgments in Isa. 5) and where atonement first touches. No one speaks for God until mouth is purged.

### Hearing is obeying: shema; waiting is braiding: qavah
Isa. 50:4: ear wakened morning by morning to hear as learned. Shema means hear-and-do. Isa. 40:31: they that wait (qavah) shall renew strength. Qavah is wait/hope/expect; linked perhaps to qav cord: braid-wait is a devotional image, not firm etymology. Waiting is not passive; it is binding your weakness to His strength until you mount wings.

### Contrite is crushed powder: dakka; trembling is chared
Isa. 57:15: I dwell with him that is dakka (crushed, ground) and shefal-ruach (lowly spirit) to revive. Isa. 66:2: I look to him that is ani (poor), nekheh-ruach (smitten spirit), vechared (trembling, quivering) at My word.
Clay must be crushed, wetted, wedged before it can be shaped. Trembling is not fear of punishment; it is awe that takes His word so seriously it shakes you into obedience.

### Calling is reciprocal: qara / hineni
Isa. 58:9: "tiqra... ve'amar hineni": thou shalt call, and He shall say Here I am. Isa. 6:8: Isaiah says "hineni, shelacheni": Here am I, send me. God mirrors your consecration. You say Here I am to Him; He says Here I am to you. That is face-to-face language.

### Hebrew patterns are pedagogy
- Parallelism: second line deepens first (Isa. 1:18: scarlet/crimson, snow/wool; double sin, double cleansing). Read both lines; the second tells you how to apply the first.
- Chiasmus: center is turning point. Isa. 55:6-11 centers on forsake-return-pardon. Find the center; that is your action.
- Rib (lawsuit): Isa. 1, 41-44. God sues to make you honest. Presence begins with truth in court.
- Hoy (woe): six woes in Isa. 5 plus "woe is me" in 6:5. You join the guilty, then grace lifts you. Do not skip woe to get to comfort.
- Repetition: "Comfort ye, comfort ye" (40:1), "Awake, awake" (51:9,17; 52:1). Doubled imperative = completed urgency. When Isaiah repeats, do it now and do it fully.
- Name as doctrine: Shear-jashub (she'ar yashuv, remnant shall return, Isa. 7:3). Your children, covenants, even setbacks can embody return.

## 3. What "pray as clay" means (Isa. 64:8)

> "But now, O Lord, thou art our father; we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand." — Isa. 64:8

Clay prayer surrenders the shape of the answer to the Father as Potter. After begging "rend the heavens, come down" (64:1), Isaiah surrenders the shape of the answer.

Praying as customer says: I know what I need, deliver it. Praying as lawyer says: I have kept my part, You owe me. Praying as stranger says: if You are there, prove it.

Praying as clay says:

1. You are Father: I am child, not contractor. I trust Your intent.
2. You are Potter: I am material, not designer. You decide the vessel: cup or platter, large or small, for honor or daily use.
3. I am workable: crushed (dakka), wet with tears, centered on wheel. Stop defending hardness.
4. Remake me to hold presence: not to avoid pain, but to abide glory. "Make me visitable, even if it hurts."
5. All of us: "we all are work." Include family, ward, enemies in the lump. No private perfection.

Jeremiah 18: the vessel marred in the Potter's hand, He made it again. The same lump, new shape. That is sanctification: not new material, new form.

Practice: kneel, open hands palms up, speak aloud: "Father, I am clay. I have been [name hardness: cynical, hurried, impure]. Wet me, crush me, center me. Make me a vessel that can bear [name person/calling/presence]. I accept Thy timing and Thy shape. Make me again." Then be still. Listen (shema). Write what comes. Obey one small shaping that day.

Enos prayed thus all day and night (Enos 1:4). The brother of Jared brought molten stones and asked Him to touch them (Ether 3:4-6), clay bringing work to Potter to be glorified. Joseph in Liberty (D&C 121-122) was remade by prison. Jesus in Gethsemane: not My will, but Thine: the perfect clay prayer.

## 4. The seven transformations: become visitable

Seven transformations make a people visitable, from washing at the gate to prayer that rends heaven.

### 1. Reason and wash (Isa. 1)
Diagnose: hands full of blood (1:15): harm, neglect, oppression while still offering. Vain oblations.
Do: rachatsu: wash. Cease evil, learn well, relieve, judge fatherless, plead widow (1:16-17). Come reason (1:18). Willing and obedient (1:19).
Promise: scarlet as snow. Zion redeemed with judgment.
Check: What one harm will I stop and what one good will I learn this week? Have I reasoned aloud with Him about it?

### 2. Ascend to learn (Isa. 2-4)
Diagnose: idols, loftiness, staying in valley.
Do: Go up to house (2:3). Walk in light (2:5). Let Him purge blood by spirit of burning (4:4): invite baptism of fire, not just forgiveness.
Promise: called holy, tabernacle covering (4:3,6).
Check: When am I next in temple with a written question? What idol did I leave below?

### 3. See, confess, be purged, hear, go (Isa. 6)
Diagnose: unclean lips among unclean people (6:5). Speaking before purged.
Do: Look to King, confess woe specifically, receive coal: plead atonement to touch mouth (6:7), then listen for Whom shall I send, answer Here am I (6:8).
Promise: iniquity taken, sin purged, commissioned.
Check: Have I named lips-sins (lying, mocking, coarse, unkind)? Have I asked Christ to touch them?

### 4. Sanctify Him, learn voice over Egypt (Isa. 7-10, 28-39)
Diagnose: asking Egypt before asking His mouth (30:2), fearing confederacy (8:12), trusting horses (31:1), mouth-near heart-far (29:13).
Do: Ask sign in height/depth (7:11). Sanctify Lord Himself as fear (8:13). Return-rest-quiet-confidence (30:15). Hear word behind thee (30:21). Pass 33:15 interview: walk righteously, speak uprightly, despise gain, refuse bribes, stop ears from blood, shut eyes from evil.
Promise: sanctuary (8:14), see King in beauty (33:17), highway of holiness (35:8).
Check: What Egypt did I consult first? What silence will I keep to hear Him?

### 5. Let yourself be carried (Isa. 40-48)
Diagnose: carrying gods that must be carried (46:1-2), weary, swearing not in truth (48:1).
Do: Prepare way (40:3). Wait-braid (40:31). Fear not, I with thee (41:10). Look and be saved (45:22). Remember borne from womb (46:3-4). Go forth of Babylon singing (48:20). Accept furnace as choosing (48:10).
Promise: glory revealed (40:5), shepherd gathers lambs (40:11), waters/fire with thee (43:2), blots (43:25), pours Spirit (44:3).
Check: What burden am I carrying that He wants to carry? What Babylon exit have I delayed?

### 6. Awake, be clean, put on garments, keep covenant (Isa. 49-57)
Diagnose: asleep in dust, bands on neck, touching unclean, kindling own sparks (50:11), polluting Sabbath.
Do: Morning ear (50:4), flint face (50:7). Hearken, look to rock (51:1). Awake, put on strength/beautiful garments, shake dust, loose bands (52:1-2). Depart, be clean (52:11). Confess straying (53:6), receive stripes healing (53:5). Enlarge tent, strengthen stakes (54:2). Come buy free, incline ear (55:1-3). Seek while near, forsake way/thoughts (55:6-7). Keep Sabbath, take hold covenant (56:4-6). Become contrite (57:15).
Promise: graven on palms (49:16), eye to eye (52:8), justified many (53:11), no weapon prospers (54:17), abundantly pardon (55:7), house of prayer (56:7), dwell with contrite to revive (57:15).
Check: What garment (covenant) will I wear more consciously? What band will I loose today?

### 7. Fast to loose, Sabbath to delight, pray to rend (Isa. 58-66)
Diagnose: self-pleasing fast, oppressing while seeking daily (58:2-4), iniquities separate (59:2).
Do: True fast: loose wickedness, undo burdens, free oppressed, break yokes (58:6). Turn from transgression (59:20). Arise-shine (60:1). Put on robe (61:10). Receive new name (62:2). Stand watchman (62:6). Plead lovingkindnesses, angel of presence (63:7,9). Beg rend heavens (64:1) as clay (64:8). Tremble at word (66:2).
Promise: light breaks, glory rearward, call answered Here I am (58:8-9), Redeemer to Zion (59:20), new heavens/earth (65:17), gather all see glory (66:18).
Check: Whose yoke will my next fast lift? When did I last beg for heavens to rend rather than circumstances to ease?

## 5. Daily / weekly / temple rhythm

A morning, daily, Sabbath, temple, and monthly fast rhythm rehearses the ascent in the body, not only in study.

Morning (5-10 min): waken ear. One Isaiah verse aloud. Write one “word in season” for a weary one. Pray as clay: Father, shape me today to bear Thee.

Daily: one wash (stop/do), one Egypt-check (did I ask His mouth first?), one carrying (give Him a burden).

Sabbath: delight, not restriction. No buying, much worship, temple or scripture feast, forgive debts. Rehearse sabbath-to-sabbath dwelling (66:23).

Temple: bring written question. Expect teaching of ways. Record answer. Let ordinances endow capacity to see face (D&C 84:19-22).

Monthly fast: attach to someone's bondage. Loose, then call and listen for Here I am.

## 6. What disqualifies, and what restores

Isa. 59:2: iniquities separate, hide face. Isa. 1:15: He will not hear with bloody hands. Heb. 12:14: no holiness, no seeing. D&C 76:116: no sanctification, cannot bear glory.

Restoration is always open while He may be found (55:6): return, confess straying (53:6), touch altar (6:7), be washed (1:18), be carried (46:4), be revived (57:15). "Though thou wast angry, anger turned away" (12:1). Then trust, draw water with joy (12:2-3).

If you have fallen, do not hide face. Bring letter to house as Hezekiah (37:14), turn face to wall and weep (38:2-3), let Him add life. Then guard post-grace pride as he failed to (39).

## 7. A clay prayer to use

Pray this plainly to practice clay posture, then obey one small shaping the same day.

Father, Thou art Potter, I am clay.
I have been hard: [name it: hurried lips, hard eyes, clenched hands].
Wet me with Thy Spirit, crush what is proud, center me on Thy wheel.
Thou hast said: wash, ascend, confess, sanctify Thee, let Thee carry, awake, be clean, fast to loose.
I will [name one obedience today].
Make me a vessel that can bear Thy weight and see Thy face: in temple, in neighbor, in veil rent.
Not my shape, but Thine. Not my timing, but Thine.
Make me again, for I am the work of Thy hand. In the name of Jesus Christ, who was wounded to heal me, amen.

Then be still. Write. Obey.

## 8. Thirty-day Isaiah path

Thirty days through Isaiah rehearse washing, hearing, carrying, cleansing, and indwelling to the point of Here I am.

Week 1: Wash/Ascend: Isa. 1, 2, 4, 6. Memorize 1:18; 6:8.
Week 2: Voice: Isa. 29, 30, 33, 35. Memorize 30:15; 33:17.
Week 3: Carried: Isa. 40, 43, 44, 48. Memorize 40:31; 46:4.
Week 4: Awake/Clean: Isa. 50, 52, 53, 55. Memorize 52:11; 55:6.
Capstone: Rend/Dwell: Isa. 58, 64, 65, 66. Memorize 58:9; 64:1; 66:2.

Read with 2 Nephi 11, 25 and 3 Nephi 23 open. End each sitting: what impurity? what washing? what promise? what one likened action?

Great is the Holy One of Israel in the midst of thee (Isa. 12:6). Become one He can be in the midst of: clean, contrite, trembling, awake, robed, and He will say Here I am.

## 9. Appendix: Baker-Ricks, the Psalms out of order, and the 15-step ascent

This answers: is Morales the Psalms book? No. The Psalms-order book is Baker & Ricks, *Who Shall Ascend into the Hill of the Lord? The Psalms in Israel's Temple Worship in the OT and in the Book of Mormon* (2009; 2nd ed. 2011).

### Their thesis in brief

- Psalms were not originally a private hymnbook but the libretto of an 8-day First-Temple drama at New Year / Tabernacles, depicting the Savior's mission from premortal council to triumph, renewed yearly from David/Solomon to Zedekiah (~600 BC, Lehi's departure).
- After exile, editors centralized worship, closed shrines, and rearranged the Psalms. Current order is post-exilic and liturgical, not chronological or dramatic. It must be reconstructed via form-criticism, ancient parallels (Akitu/Enuma Elish), Barker, Mowinckel, Gunkel, Nibley.
- Proof text is Psalm 24: procession circling the city redefining it as New Jerusalem/Zion, then gate/veil interrogation: *Who shall ascend? He that hath clean hands and pure heart* (24:3-4), then *Lift up your heads, O ye gates, and the King of glory shall come in* (24:7-10).
- Drama logic: creation → fall → atonement → veil → crowning → lecture → feast. The king as Adam/Israel/Jehovah surrogate is washed, anointed, clothed, given a new king-name, adopted as son, lectures the Law from the throne, enters the Holy of Holies (perfect cube/throne), feet established. Royal psalms (2, 21, 22, 45, 72, 82, 110, 132) are double: reigning king *and* future Christ.
- Psalm 23 as microcosm in 3 acts: green pastures/still waters (premortal), valley/table/oil/cup (mortal), dwell in house forever (eternal).
- Full arc they reconstruct: Council (Ps 82) → Wedding (Ps 45) → Moses/Law → Davidic covenant → Combat vs chaos/death → Jehovah conquers → Triumph procession (Ps 24:1-2) → Coronation (Ps 2; Isa 61) → Veil (Ps 15/24 + 25/21) → Holy of Holies ascent → Establishing feet → Lecture → 8th-day feast/return to Eden.
- Brass plates kept the pre-exilic form; Book of Mormon preserves it: Nephi's review, Benjamin's coronation, Abinadi's kingship, Alma 5/12-13 invitation to rest, 3 Nephi as actualized drama (coming/coronation/sermon/salt-light), Moroni 7.

Use this as lens, not dogma: the authors call it opinion, not revelation about modern temples. But the pattern matches Isaiah and the Songs of Ascent strikingly.

### The 15 Songs of Ascent as your staircase (Ps 120-134)

Tradition links them to 15 temple steps, 15 words of the priestly blessing (Num. 6:24-26), Hezekiah's 15 years. Journey: distress far off → Keeper → glad arrival → supplication → deliverance → stability → joy → house/family → endurance → depths → humility → ark rest → anointing → night blessing.

Senses sanctified: lying tongue (120) → lifted eyes (121,123) → standing feet in gates (122:2).

### How this maps to your Isaiah manual

- Isa 2 (go up) = Ps 122 glad arrival. Start ascent glad, not grim.
- Isa 6 (woe/coal/send) = Ps 120 woe + 130 depths + 131 child. Confess, be purged, quiet.
- Isa 35 highway = Ps 120-134 whole road. Unclean shall not pass, fools shall not err: wayfaring redeemed walk singing.
- Isa 52 awake/clean/eye-to-eye = Ps 132 arise to rest + 133 anointing + 134 night ministry. Put on garments, receive oil, become watchman who blesses.
- Isa 58 fast that brings Here I am = Ps 133-134 unity that commands blessing. Loosed yokes create oil-flow; oil-flow creates night light.

Baker-Ricks Act order and Isaiah agree: gate questions (Ps 15/24; Isa 33:14-17 who shall dwell with fire?) come *late*, after combat/deliverance, just before veil/anointing/crowning. You do not answer them by study; you become the answer by traversing 120-131 first.

### Practice: 15 days, 15 steps, 5 Isaiah anchors

Days 1-3 (leave): Ps 120-122 + Isa 1-2. Write: what Meshech am I in? What hill do I lift eyes to? When am I glad to go up?
Days 4-6 (hold): Ps 123-125 + Isa 30, 40. Pray servant-eyes, list floods escaped, declare stability verse.
Days 7-9 (home): Ps 126-128 + Isa 54, 65. Sow one tearful prayer, let Him build one home habit.
Days 10-12 (depths): Ps 129-131 + Isa 50, 53, 57. Confess (130), quiet as child (131), receive healing (53:5).
Days 13-15 (dwell/bless): Ps 132-134 + Isa 52, 61-62, 58. Seek rest, receive unity, stand one night-watch to bless someone, then record your Here I am.

End with Ps 24 aloud at a temple gate, real or pictured: *Who shall ascend? ... Lift up your heads...* Answer with Isa 6:8 and 58:9: *Here am I, send me. Thou hast said Here I am to me.*

## 10. Parallel deep dive: all 15 days with more Isaiah anchors

Each of the fifteen ascent psalms pairs with an Isaiah anchor for daily practice. For each day: psalm stage, primary Isaiah anchor, more anchors that repeat the pattern, Hebrew note, practice, memorize verse.

### Days 1-3: Leave Babylon glad

**Day 1: Ps 120 exile-honest.** Woe in Meshech/Kedar, lying lips, I am for peace (120:5-7). Primary: Isa 1:16 wash you, make you clean + 6:5 woe is me undone unclean lips. More: Isa 59:3 lips defiled, 59:12-13 lying, 29:13 mouth-near heart-far. Hebrew: *woe (hoy)* joins you to guilty before grace lifts; *rachats* wash is reflexive: step in. Practice: name one lie/deception to wash today; pray: Father, I am in Meshech, wash my lips. Memorize Ps 120:7 / Isa 1:18.

**Day 2: Ps 121 Keeper.** Lift eyes to hills, help from Maker heaven-earth, He never slumbers, shomer 6x (121:1-8). Primary: Isa 40:28-31 He faints not, wait-renew wings + 2:2 mountain established top of mountains. More: Isa 4:5-6 tabernacle covering/shade, 27:3 keep day and night, 52:7 feet on mountains publish. Hebrew: *shamar* keeper, *tsel* shade/tabernacle, *alah* up. Practice: list what you self-keep; let Him keep feet/soul 24h; pray: Keeper, shadow me. Memorize Ps 121:1-2.

**Day 3: Ps 122 glad arrival.** I was glad, let us go to house, feet standing in gates, pray shalom Jerusalem, compact together (122:1-8). Primary: Isa 2:3 come let us go up to house, He will teach ways + 2:5 walk in light. More: Isa 11:9 no hurt in holy mountain, 62:6-7 set watchmen pray, 66:10 rejoice with Jerusalem. Hebrew: *alah* go up, *shalom/shalem* wholeness wordplay, *yachdav* knit. Practice: pray for ward/city/Israel peace by name; send peacemaking text; pray: set my feet, make me peacemaker. Memorize Ps 122:1.

Synthesis 1-3: conviction → trust → joy. Wash, kept, compacted. Scarlet to snow to song.

### Days 4-6: Hold: low gaze to stability

**Day 4: Ps 123 servant eyes.** Eyes to Dweller in heavens as servants to master's hand until mercy (123:1-4). Primary: Isa 30:18-19 Lord waits to be gracious, blessed waiters. More: Isa 8:17 wait when face hid, 45:22 look and be saved, 40:31 wait-renew. Hebrew: *avadim/shiphchah* servants, *enayim el-yad* eyes to hand, *chanan* stoop-mercy, *qavah* braid-wait. Practice: 3x lift eyes 60 sec silence before petition. Memorize Ps 123:2.

**Day 5: Ps 124 escaped flood.** If not Lord on our side, waters overwhelmed soul, snare broken escaped as bird, help in name Maker (124:1-8). Primary: Isa 43:1-3 pass waters/rivers not overflow, I with thee. More: Isa 8:7-8 Assyria flood, 59:19 Spirit lifts standard vs flood, 41:10 fear not I uphold. ## 11. Temple fill: leaving Babylon, feast, Hezekiah, decoders, D&C/BoM presence

*Scripture only. No modern commentary. Temple symbols for becoming visitable.*

Temple symbols in Isaiah train leaving Babylon, entering the feast, praying as Hezekiah prayed, and abiding presence through ordinances.

### 11A. Go out from Babylon: become humble (Isa 47, 42, 14)

False throne: "Come down, sit in dust... there is no throne" (Isa 47:1). "Thou sayest... I am, and none else" (47:8,10): stealing God's *ani hu* (43:10). Sorceries/enchantments cannot save (47:9,11). Forsake 3 spells by name: assurance-scrolling, security-hoarding, image-craft. "Go ye out... be ye clean that bear vessels" (D&C 133:5; 38:42; Alma 5:57).

Servant gentleness: "A bruised reed shall he not break, smoking flax shall he not quench... covenant... light... open blind eyes... bring prisoners" (Isa 42:1-7). "I will bring blind by way they knew not... not forsake" (42:16). Rule: never finish whom Babylon wounded. Mend privately, fan flickering faith, lead kindly.

Pride root vs dwell-with: "I will ascend... exalt throne... be like most High" (14:13-14) vs "I dwell... with him of contrite humble spirit, to revive" (57:15). "Lofty looks humbled... Lord alone exalted" (2:11). "Arrogancy cease" (13:11). Nightly examen: where I-am-none-else? whom crushed? what unknown way led?

Memorize Isa 42:3; 57:15.

### 11B. Gate, feast, Person (Isa 56, 25, 63)

Gate open: "Keep judgment... keepeth sabbath from polluting... hand from evil" (56:1-2). Stranger/eunuch not separated: "in mine house... place and name better... everlasting name" (56:5). "Bring to holy mountain... joyful in house of prayer... house of prayer for all people" (56:7). Sabbath plan: lay down one work, do one justice, delight meal/sing/testimony. Monthly stranger-seat: invite one far-off, read 56:7, name them in prayer.

Feast unveiled: "In this mountain... feast fat things... destroy covering cast... vail spread... swallow death... wipe tears... Lo, this is our God; we have waited" (25:6-9). Link 35:10 singing return, 27:3 keep/water vineyard night and day. Sunday table as mountain: best food, read 25:6-9, sing 35:10, name tears.

Person present: "Lovingkindnesses... angel of his presence saved them... bare them, carried" (63:7,9). "They rebelled, vexed holy Spirit" (63:10). "Redeemer shall come to Zion, unto them that turn... My spirit... words... shall not depart" (59:20-21). "Lord shall be everlasting light" (60:19). *Chesed* loyalty, *malak-panav* messenger of Face, *or* light. Examen: where vexed Spirit? Turn + memorize one verse to keep word in mouth.

Memorize Isa 56:7; 25:8.

### 11C. Hezekiah: clay answered (Isa 36-39)

Besieged/spread: "Hezekiah... rent clothes, sackcloth, went into house" (37:1). "Received letter... spread it before Lord... Thou art God alone... save us... that kingdoms may know" (37:14-20). Gate silence first: "Answer him not" (36:21). Take accuser-letter up, spread whole, end *Thou alone*. Write one-page Rabshakeh letter, spread physically, pray 37:16-20.

Sick/weep: "Turned face toward wall, prayed... wept sore" (38:2-3). "Heard prayer, seen tears: add 15 years... deliver" (38:5-6). "Cast all sins behind back" (38:17). "Sing songs in house all days" (38:20). Wall = Holy Place, back to world. Tears libation. After spread, turn to wall 5 min, write added mercy, sing one hymn.

Healed/guarded: "Shewed house of precious things... nothing not shewed" (39:2). "All... shall be carried to Babylon" (39:6). "Good... peace in my days" (39:8). Mercy unguarded becomes tour. No prayer before showing. On same paper add: Mercy added ___ / Guard I vow ___ (spread only before Lord, pray before showing).

Memorize Isa 37:20.
