# Scribal changes catalog — what else the scribes changed (2026-09-08)

Companion to purity-patterns.md §6. Only documented variants with loci.
Legend: direction A→B means oldest recoverable → later standardization.
Tier0 impact noted where a BIND row is touched.

## A. Reverence edits (euphemism, name avoidance)

1. Tiqqune sopherim — traditional 18 "corrections of the scribes" (Masorah;
exact list varies by source; substance stable): Gen 18:22 (YHWH stood before
Abraham → Abraham stood before YHWH); Num 11:15; Num 12:12; 1 Sam 3:13 (sons
blasphemed God → themselves); 2 Sam 16:12; 1 Kgs 12:16; 2 Chr 10:16;
Jer 2:11; Ezek 8:17; Hos 4:7; Hab 1:12 ("You do not die" → "we shall not
die" — God cannot die); Zech 2:12[8]; Mal 1:13; Ps 106:20; Job 1:5,11;
Job 2:5,9 (see #2). Pattern: remove anthropomorphism or blasphemy-adjacent
phrasing about God. Purity: none touch Tier0 sentences; they touch the
YHWH-portrait edges (Hab 1:12 bears on "holy, cannot die/look on evil").
2. `barak` = bless/curse euphemism: Job 1:5,11; 2:5,9; 1 Kgs 21:10,13 (Naboth).
"Curse God" written as "bless God." Affects Satan-dialogue and trial scenes;
does not change Tier0 James 1:13 control.
3. Adonai-vocalization: YHWH pointed with Adonai/Elohim vowels (qere
perpetuum); ~300 `YHWH Elohim` combos pointed as Elohim. Affects Name
pronunciation, not doctrine; Pike control stands.
4. Ex 24:9-11 theophany: MT elders "saw God, and ate and drank" retained;
LXX/Targums soften with "place where God stood" glosses in reception.
No MT change; reception-softening noted for Ex 33:11/20 mechanism file.

## B. Polemical edits (name desecration, place slurs)

5. Baal → bosheth (shame): Ish-baal → Ish-bosheth (2 Sam 2:8/4:12);
Merib-baal → Mephibosheth (2 Sam 4:4/9:6); Jerubbaal → Jerubbesheth
(2 Sam 11:21). Direction: theophoric Baal element shamed post-reform.
6. Bethel → Beth-aven (house of wickedness): Hos 4:15; 5:8; 10:5 (cf. Josh
7:2 Aven near Bethel). Northern royal sanctuary delegitimized in text.
7. Asherah handling: 2 Kgs 23 purge inventory keeps the word (vessels,
poles cut down) while cultic function is condemned; Cohn Anat-only caution
stands — no text renames her out, unlike bosheth pattern.

## C. Theological smoothing (council, sons, agency)

8. Deut 32:8 sons of God → sons of Israel (MT) vs LXX angels vs 4QDeutj
bene elohim. Oldest = sons of God. Purity: TRUST council row depends on DSS.
9. Deut 32:43 expanded call (4QDeutj + LXX: heavens/elohim/Gentiles praise)
vs MT short. Quoted Heb 1:6 — NT follows expansion, not MT.
10. Ps 8:5 elohim → LXX/Heb 2:7 "angels" ("a little lower than angels").
Lowers divine-council reading of man's status; Hebrews follows LXX.
11. 2 Sam 24:1 anger of YHWH moves David vs 1 Chr 21:1 Satan provokes.
Chronicler theologizes agency (post-exilic Satan office). Kept both;
purity USE-background, vetoed where contradicting enemy-love.
12. Isa 53:11 "see light": 1QIsaa + LXX add "light" ("see light, be
satisfied") vs MT lacks object ("he shall see"). Affects Servant vindication
reading; 1QIsaa sides with LXX against MT.
13. Job 7:20?/42:6? — Masoretic plus ketiv/qere softening tradition around
"despise/comfort" (debated; flagged, not scored).

## D. Redactional editions and expansions

14. Jeremiah two editions: LXX ~1/7 shorter, oracles-against-nations placed
differently (after 25 vs chs 46-51). MT expanded (Baruch-era additions).
Direction: growth, not cut. Purity: Jeremiah martyrdom traditions stay
link-only.
15. 1 Samuel: LXX lacks 17:12-31,55-58 (David intro/Goliath extras);
4QSam-a adds Nahash paragraph before 1 Sam 11 (siege terms). Elhanan killed
Goliath 2 Sam 21:19 → 1 Chr 20:5 "brother of Goliath" (harmonizing gloss).
16. Chronicles retelling: Manasseh repentance only in 2 Chr 33 (absent Kings);
census numbers inflated vs Samuel/Kings; Levi/priest interests foregrounded.
17. Samaritan Pentateuch: Ex 20 // Deut 5 harmonized; Deut 27:4 Ebal →
Gerizim (sectarian mountain); ~6,000 mostly harmonistic variants.
Direction: expansion + sectarian claim. Purity: background only.
18. LXX additions: Esther additions, Daniel additions (Susannah, Bel, Prayer
of Azariah), 1 Esdras, Psalm 151. Canon-boundary material; D&C 91 lens.

## E. Chronology and numbers

19. Gen 5 / 11 patriarchal ages: MT vs LXX (+100 yrs pre-flood begetting ages;
LXX Methuselah survives flood problem) vs SP (different again). No consensus
original; direction debated (Samaritan/LXX inflation vs MT deflation theories
both live). Purity: background; no Tier0 touch.
20. Ahaziah 22 (2 Kgs 8:26) vs 42 (2 Chr 22:2); Jehoiachin 8 vs 18
(2 Chr 36:9 vs 2 Kgs 24:8). Copyist numerals; Chronicles side usually
secondary. Background.
21. Census/battle numbers Samuel-Kings vs Chronicles (e.g., horsemen,
shekels). Chronicles inflation pattern; background.

## F. NT major variants (purity-relevant flagged)

22. Mark 16:9-20 longer ending: absent earliest (Sinaiticus/Vaticanus);
commission + signs + ascension. Purity: resurrection witness affected;
rank TRUST-with-cluster (1 Cor 15 list carries weight independently).
23. John 7:53-8:11 pericope adulterae: absent earliest; floats (after Luke
21 in some). Forgiveness portrait unaffected (Luke 7/15 carry it); passage
USE, not BIND.
24. 1 John 5:7 Comma Johanneum: late Trinitarian gloss, no Greek before
~14th c. Trinity proof-texting must not rest here (purity: Reject as base).
25. Matt 6:13 doxology ("kingdom, power, glory"): liturgical addition;
earliest lack it. Lord's Prayer BIND rows unaffected (petition core stable).
26. John 1:18 "only begotten God" (P66/75/Aleph) vs "only begotten Son"
(later). Earliest harder reading favors God-language; JST 1:19 resolves via
record-of-Son. Purity: note, no score change.
27. 1 Tim 3:16 "God was manifest" vs "who/which was manifest" (theos vs hos,
one-letter uncial confusion ΘΣ/ΟΣ). Godhead proof-texting caution.
28. Luke 23:34a "Father, forgive them": absent some early witnesses.
Enemy-love control does NOT depend on it (Matt 5:44 + Rom 5 carry).
29. Matt 27:16-17 "Jesus Barabbas": early witnesses include Jesus- prefix
(Origen notes). indeterminant; background.
30. Rev 13:18 666 vs 616 (P115/C). Number-symbol caution; background.

## G. Purity impact summary

- BIND rows touched: #24 (must not use Comma), #25 (doxology not core),
  #26-27 (note variants, scores stand), #12 (1QIsaa strengthens Servant row).
- TRUST rows touched: #22 (Mark ending — cluster with 1 Cor 15), #23
  (adulterae — cluster with Luke), #15 (Goliath — cluster, not base).
- Background only: everything in D and E, plus #5-7 polemics.
- Rule restated: single-verse identity claims (YHWH=Satan, Comma-Trinity)
  die on variants; theme-clusters (bread, forgive, no-tempt, enemy-love,
  known-via-Son) survive across MT/LXX/DSS/NT/JST.
