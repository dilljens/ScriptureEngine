## Sumerian (live ETCSL fetches, short excerpts)

- Flood/Ziusudra (t.1.7.4): decision + pious king + 7-day boat + sun/window +
  sacrifice shape shared; motive (assembly oath-politics, no reason stated),
  mechanism (Enki ventriloquizes wall to dodge oath vs direct "God said to
  Noah"), outcome (Dilmun apotheosis-escape vs rainbow covenant + seedtime
  promise) inverted.
- Enki/Ninhursag (t.1.1.1): paradise waters, rib-life pun Ninti (`ti` = rib/
  life :: havvah/hay), eating-curse-healing shared; motive (predatory incest
  chain) vs covenant love inverted. Enki tempts — Tier0 violation by name.
- Enmerkar (t.1.8.2.3): one-tongue spell + ambitious kings + writing-tech
  shared; vector opposite (unity-as-imperial-liturgy vs disunity-as-merciful-
  judgment). Inverted function.
- Shuruppak (t.5.6.1): father-son frame + pre-flood hero's father + theft/
  adultery/surety/speech prohibitions shared with Prov/Decalogue; theocentric
  root absent ("fear of LORD"), closes to Nisaba; slave ethic (rootless
  exploitable foreigner) vs Deut 24:14/Prov 14:31. Shape shared, ethic hollow.
- Inanna descent (t.1.4.1): 7-gate stripping, hook corpse, 3-day sprinkled
  revival, shepherd substitute — narrative grammar of Hos 6:2/1 Cor 15 with
  motive (throne-craving seizure), mechanism (dirt-creature flattery trick),
  ethic (betrays Dumuzid to save self) inverted. Textbook counterfeit
  resurrection.

## Hindu (vendored PD)

- Nasadiya 10.129: waters/darkness + gods-later shared with Gen 1:2; no
  speaker/command/good, ends agnostic ("perhaps he knows not") vs fiat +
  Job 38 self-disclosure. Contradictory epistemology.
- Purusha 10.90: body-part cosmos parallels Enuma carcase (not Gen); varna
  anatomy contradicts image-equality and impartial giving. Incompatible.
- Matsya SB 1.8.1: warning + vessel + horn-tow + mountain + lone Manu +
  Ida repopulation — real ark-shape memory with no judgment, no covenant,
  fish-helper instead of YHWH. Formal parallel, theological antithesis.
- Manu 1: seed-waters-egg-bisection + varna + austerity demiurges vs fiat,
  blessing, grace. Incompatible cosmogony/anthropology.
- Chandogya 6: "one only without second" + absorption (rivers lose riverhood,
  salt unseen) vs Creator-creature distinction + communion-without-confusion.
  Monistic contrary; no forgiveness/cross/enemy-love.
- (Link) Yugas cyclic decline vs Hesiod ages + Dan 2 linear-eschatological.

## Net pattern (extends purity-patterns §1-2)

All three extend the rule: genuine memories (beginning, waters, first pair,
flood/enclosure, lie, fire-assay, renewal) systematically recast — agnostic
origin for fiat, dismembered/oviparous hierarchy for image-equality, technique
survival for judged covenant, absorption or dualist timetable for
Father-Word renewal. Formal parallels, contrast verdicts throughout.
Strongest forms: Matsya flood-shape, vara ark-shape, Ninti/havvah pun,
one-tongue spell, 3-day sprinkled revival. Sharpest inversions: Enki
predation, Inanna betrayal-substitute, varna anatomy, eugenic vara,
mechanical molten universalism.
