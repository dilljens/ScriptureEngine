# What 3 rounds got us + learnings (2026-09-09)

## Round 1 — Brenton LXX + Didache + Josephus Apion
Got: Deut 32:8 "angels of God" + 32:9 "portion" and Ps 8:5 "less than angels"
verified in 1851 print at source (CCEL Psalms gap closed by full-text file);
Didache ch.7 trine formula + ch.8 doxology ("for thine is the power and the
glory for ever" — earliest doxology witness, 1st c.); Apion 1.38-41 22-book
canon ("twenty-two books... contain the records") + Essenes.
Learned: liturgy-into-text is 1st-century normal (doxology), which reframes
insertion polemics — distinguish early-liturgical (doxology) from
late-dogmatic (Comma). Canon-as-recognition now has a 1st-c. witness, not
just Athanasius 367.

## Round 2 — Charles APOT + Targums + Philo
Got: full 1913 corpus local (6.3 + 4.5MB OCR; "enoch" 856 hits in vol.2) —
future Second-Temple queries answerable offline; Onkelos Ex 24 "Glory" + Ex 33
Shekinah/Memra buffers word-verified (mechanism file now primary, not
secondary); Philo Conf. 62-63 East/firstborn verified (John-1 background
without dependence — Philo's Logos never incarnate, never forgives: Tier0
separator holds).
Learned: buffers are 2nd-century, not medieval apologetics; Logos vocabulary
predates John yet John inverts its use (Word becomes flesh vs Word stays
idea). Biggest open paths: Peshitta critical English (none PD), KTU English
(paywalled), Gathas English (browser-only gap stands).
