## 2. Before/after: prophets expanding their understanding

**Nephi — before:** 1 Ne 19:23 (liken), 2 Ne 11:2–4 (eyewitness certification: Isaiah
"saw my Redeemer even as I have seen him" + Jacob also), 11:8 (rejoice), 25:1–8
(four keys: manner-difficulty, no rabbinic shortcut, spirit-of-prophecy key,
latter-day lens). **After:** 25:19–30 (Messiah in 600 years; law dead without Christ;
"we talk of Christ, rejoice... preach... prophesy... that our children may know");
26–27 (Isa 29 → sealed book, Gentile churches, "a Bible! a Bible!"); 28–30
(gathering, no Gentile/Jew exclusion). Pattern: *certify → quote → christologize →
gather.* Nephi's keys map 1:1 onto our method doc (difficulty admitted, spirit as
key, double time-frame = veil seeing).

**Jacob — before:** 2 Ne 6 (covenant gathering frame, "the Lord... will... gather").
**After (ch. 9):** the infinite atonement (9:5–26: no flesh could satisfy justice;
God Himself suffers; resurrection passes upon all; "O the greatness... O the
wisdom"); ch. 10 (reconcile, no kings, "this land... unto the remnant"). Pattern:
*quote servant → prove infinite price → command reconciliation.* Jacob turns Isa
49–52 into atonement arithmetic.

**Abinadi — before:** priests deploy Isa 52:7–10 as a gotcha (12:20–24: "what mean
these words?"). **After:** the law still binds but cannot save (12:25–13);
Decalogue rehearsed (13); Isa 53 quoted whole (14); then the exposition no one
expected — 15:1 "God himself shall come down... and redeem," seed = believers,
bands loosed (15:8–9), and 16 (resurrection, rebellion unforgiven without
intercession). Pattern: *their proof-text → his whole chapter → identity of the
Sufferer.* Abinadi answers *who* publishes peace (52:7) with *who* bears stripes (53)
with *who* descends (15:1): one answer, three depths.

**Christ — before:** theophany + sacrament + unity prayer (11–19; note the 19:23,29
unity petitions = John 17 grammar our temple essay graded HIGH). 3 Ne 20: covenant
explained, Father's work, Gentile remnant, "they shall see eye to eye" (20:32).
**Quotes:** Isa 52 watchmen/arm (16:18–20) and Isa 54 whole (22). **After:** the
command (23:1–3), the record-test (23:6–13: "these scriptures ye had not," Samuel
added, "I commanded... it should be written"), then "he did expound all things...
great and small" (26:1). Pattern: *embody → quote → command → examine the record →
expound everything.* The resurrected Jesus treats Isaiah as homework with a quiz on
completeness.

**Moroni — after only:** 10:30–33 compresses Isa 52:1–2 into awake/shake/deny/love/
come/perfected-unto-spotlessness — the book's last exhortation is Isaiah's reveille
fused to grace ("denied of ungodliness... perfected in Christ by grace"). Pattern:
*the final word is Isaiah's first word (awake) finished by Christ's last word (grace).*

## 3. What Jesus said (3 Nephi + NT control)

**The command (23:1–3) gives three reasons, and each maps onto our detectors:**
1. "Great are the words" — *kavod*, weight (essay §3). Greatness = mass to be borne.
2. "All things concerning my people... of the house of Israel; therefore... also to
   the Gentiles" — the **goy double-sense** (Janus-13: 9:2, 49:7, 19:24): Israel-text
   that must speak Gentile because the Servant is the nations' light.
3. "All things... have been and shall be" — **veil temporality** (past+future on one
   screen; 46:10; detector typology rows): Isaiah sees whole because he stood where
   time is visible.

**What He chose to voice:** watchmen singing together (52:8 ×2: 16:18, 20:32),
the bare arm in all nations' eyes (16:20 = 52:10 — our Arm twin-pair's servant-side
hub 52:10!), and the barren mother (54). Not woes: *recognition scenes* — the
moments Israel sees what was hidden. His Isaiah is the Isaiah of unveiling.

**NT control (same voice, earlier):** Matt 13:14–15 cites Isa 6:9–10 for judicial
deafness (our curriculum's hear/deafen pair); John 12:38–41 cites Isa 53:1 + 6:10
then adds "these things said Esaias, when he saw **his glory**, and spake of him" —
John reads Isa 6 as a Christophany, the First-Temple claim in canonical dress;
Luke 4:18–19 takes Isa 61:1–2 (the Melchizedek/Jubilee text) as His commission;
Matt 12:17–21 takes Isa 42:1–4 (bruised reed — our audited Janus 42:4); Matt 8:17
takes 53:4 (borne sicknesses); Paul (Rom 9–11) takes remnant texts (1:9; 10:22) and
52:7/53:1. Jesus and the apostles quote Isaiah's *hinges*, never his furniture.

## 4. New patterns the BoM lens reveals

1. **Boundary-quotation habit:** every block spans a judgment→comfort or
   suffering→covenant seam — the prophets segment Isaiah exactly where our
   reversals/days segment him. Independent confirmation of the DB structure.
2. **Scattering→gathering frame, every time:** 1 Ne 22 (mighty Gentile nation
   nursing fathers), 2 Ne 30 (no exclusion), Jacob 6/10, Abinadi 15:18 (beautiful
   feet publish), 3 Ne 20–22, Moro 10 (gather to Christ). The *after* is always
   gathering — Isaiah's end-state in every witness.
3. **1 Ne 21:1's re-pointing** is a licensed hermeneutic on display: address changed
   *inside* the quote (isles→exiled house) because the goy double-sense permits it.
   Our twin-pair table is the grammar of exactly this move.
4. **Abinadi's who-ladder** (52:7 publisher → 53 sufferer → 15:1 God himself) is the
   servant-identity question *answered in three depths* — use against the
   Davidic-Servant debate: whatever the end-time servant is, the Sufferer is God.
5. **Christ's record-test (23:6–13)** makes completeness a doctrine: missing Samuel
   must be written; "expound all things" follows. A claim about ancient roots should
   be testable *and complete* — our Yoma/Tamid control and fetch lists are the
   current "ye had not" items; name them as such.
6. **Nephi's 25:1–8 is a method paper** matching our pipeline: admit difficulty
   (audit), refuse shortcuts (detectors don't replace the Spirit), use the
   Christ-key, read latter-day (double time-frame). He would approve of `proposed`.
