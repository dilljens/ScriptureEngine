# From Pattern to Conclusion — Method, Then the Path to Come Unto God

Two questions answered: (1) exactly how each conclusion was drawn from each pattern,
worked step by step; (2) what else the patterns yield — Isaiah's practical curriculum
for coming unto God. Companion to `isaiah-message-from-patterns.md` (the nine sentences).

---

## PART 1 — How pattern becomes conclusion (three worked examples)

The pipeline is always the same five steps:

**Detect** (formal, countable, no theology) → **Audit** (hand-read samples, score fit,
fix bugs) → **Function** (what does this device DO to a reader, per scholarship?) →
**Converge** (do independent patterns agree?) → **Conclude** (state it, with the
confidence of the weakest link, and what would overturn it).

### Worked example A: the outstretched hand (epiphora detector)

1. **Detect.** Rule: 3-word closing shingles repeating 3+ times in one chapter (Berlin's
   epiphora). It fired on "stretched out still" chaining 9:12→17→21.
2. **Audit.** Read all three: each ends a different judgment stanza (Syrians devouring,
   hypocrites/evil-doers, Ephraim-vs-Manasseh civil war) with the identical full line:
   "For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still."
   6/6 epiphora samples fit. (Detector also found 45's "none else" and 64's "at thy
   presence" — same mechanism, different chapters.)
3. **Function.** An epiphora is a *closer*: it tells the hearer what the stanza was for.
   Repeating the SAME closer after DIFFERENT judgments forces one verdict: the
   judgments differ, the hand doesn't. Kugel's rule applies — the second and third
   occurrences aren't repetition, they're escalation: after worse rebellion, the hand is
   *still* out. And עוֹד (9:11 pivot, Janus table) cuts both ways: man's "again"
   (rebuild in defiance, 9:10) vs God's "still" (hand extended, 9:12).
4. **Converge.** Independent rows agree: Gileadi's 11 curse→blessing reversals (every
   judgment panel has a deliverance twin); servant promises that the bruised reed is
   not broken (42:3); "mine anger... but with everlasting kindness will I have mercy"
   (54:7–8).
5. **Conclude (message sentence 1).** Judgment is real and mercy outlasts it — stated
   at detector-grade confidence for chs. 9–10, extended book-wide only via the
   converging rows. **Overturned if:** the refrain proves redactional padding with no
   rhetorical function — but its placement (after each escalation cliff) is exactly
   where closers do work, so this is unlikely.

### Worked example B: Assyria the beloved (Janus detector)

1. **Detect.** Rule: a mid-frequency polysemous lemma (lexicon multi-sense entry)
   whose neighboring verses each match a DIFFERENT sense's vocabulary, uniquely per
   side. It fired on אַשּׁוּר (Assyria) pivoting 19:24, neighbors 19:23/19:25.
2. **Audit.** 19:23: highway Egypt↔Assyria, former enemies serving together. 19:24:
   "Israel... the third with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing." 19:25: "Blessed be Egypt
   my people, and Assyria the work of my hands, and Israel mine inheritance." The
   SAME name moves enemy → partner → beloved across three verses — verified by
   reading, FIT.
3. **Function.** Janus (Rendsburg/Watson) is a *pivot*: one word that belongs to both
   sides and converts between them. Here the conversion IS the theology: the oppressor's
   name, unaltered, gets re-pointed from wrath-target to covenant-child. Form performs
   content — grace renames without respelling.
4. **Converge.** goy-pivots agree (9:2 nations see light; 49:6–8 Gentiles' light);
   Cyrus named-and-claimed (44:28–45:6); "princes... join his enemies" reversed into
   kings worshipping (49:7); Rahab/Egypt4824? no — stay with rows: 11Q13-adjacent
   Melchizedek expectation and the 45 "none else" seal.
5. **Conclude (inside message sentence 5).** The nations are both rod and harvest;
   no enemy-name is frozen — stated at *proposed* confidence (Janus stays proposed by
   house rule), upgraded toward firm by the goy-cluster repeating it 4+ times.
   **Overturned if:** 19:24–25 proves late gloss with no Isaianic root — possible, and
   would demote this instance without touching the 9:2/49:7 pivots.

### Worked example C: left → washed → fire-cloud (tercet detector)

1. **Detect.** Rule: three consecutive same-chapter verses sharing rare lemmas
   (freq ≤25) in all three pairs. Fired on 4:3–4–5 (shared12=2, shared23=1, shared13=2).
2. **Audit.** 4:3 "he that is left in Zion... shall be called holy"; 4:4 "when the Lord
   shall have washed away the filth... purged the blood... by the spirit of judgment
   and burning"; 4:5 "the LORD will create upon... mount Zion... a cloud and smoke
   by day, and flaming fire by night." Read straight: remnant-status → washing →
   Sinai-cloud restored over the assembly. 5/5 aaa-samples fit book-wide.
3. **Function.** Tercet 2+1/1+2 (Alter): the third colon *arrives*. The unit teaches by
   escalation — status, cleansing, presence — and the rare-lemma glue (Zion/wash/
   create) proves the poet bound them, not the redactor.
4. **Converge.** Tabernacle-ladder rows (laver→cloud), 1:16–18 (wash→reason→snow),
   Alma 13 / 3 Ne 19 sanctification parallels, essay §§5–7 stations.
5. **Conclude.** Cleansing is ordered and ends in cloud, not in Court — stated at
   detector-grade for the unit, typological beyond it. **Overturned if:** shared
   lemmas prove common-topic coincidence — mitigated by the ≤25 rarity gate plus the
   5/5 audit.

**The trust rule that falls out:** believe a device at its audit score; believe a
*message* where ≥2 independent devices converge; keep Janus/inclusio/sound as
`proposed` witnesses, never lone proof.

## PART 2 — What else: Isaiah's curriculum for coming unto God

Mining the invitation verbs (counts are live queries, book of Isaiah) plus the
validated patterns gives a staged path — each stage is a verse, a verb-count, and a
pattern that stages it:

0. **Be reasoned with (1:18, hapax command).** The lawsuit (*rib*) opens the book:
   "come now, and let us reason together." God litigates before He washes — honesty
   is the gate (essay §13 gate psalms). Pattern: covenant-lawsuit frame of ch. 1.
1. **Wash, cease, learn (1:16–17, each once — said once because once is enough).**
   "Wash you... cease to do evil; learn to do well." Stop-word and start-word in one
   breath. Pattern: tercet logic in a single verse (negative, negative, positive).
2. **Hear — 127x, the master practice.** "Hear, O heavens" (1:2) to "he that heareth...
   shall live" via 55:3 ("hear, and your soul shall live"). Counter-pattern: the
  xxxii judicial deafening of 6:9–10 — hearing refused becomes hearing removed.
   Fifteen *hearken* imperatives (28:23 "give ye ear, and hear my voice" is the
   purest). **If Isaiah had one exercise, it is this: daily wakened ear (50:4).**
3. **Wait — 12x, climaxing 40:31.** 8:17 (hide-face season) → 25:9 ("we have waited
   for him") → 30:18 ("blessed are all they that wait") → 33:2 → 40:31 (eagle wings).
   Pattern: the wait-verses sit at furnace points (affliction 30, exile 40, siege 33) —
   waiting is what you do *in* the furnace, and the stance that gets wings.
4. **Trust + fear-not (26:4 once-stated; "fear not" 8x: 7:4, 35:4, 41:13–14, 43:1,5,
   44:2, 54:4).** Transfer the burden to the Carrier (46:3–4: idols carried, God
   carries). Pattern: every "fear not" answers a named terror (fire, worm-Jacob,
   exile, barrenness) — courage is always *addressed*, never abstract.
5. **Awake, arise, shine (5 + 10 + 3).** Zion asleep in dust gets triple "awake"
   (51:9 arm, 51:17 cup, 52:1 garments) then "arise, shine" (60:1). Pattern: the
   awakening tercet — call the arm, drain the cup, put on the clothes. You dress
   *before* you feel awake.
6. **Depart pure (52:11, once).** "Depart ye... touch no unclean thing; be ye clean,
   that bear the vessels." Exodus verbs (go forth 48:20 → depart 52:11) with a
   purity gate. Pattern: inclusio of the Servant section — vessels carried out clean.
7. **Thirst, drink free (55:1–3).** "Ho, every one that thirsteth... without money...
   incline your ear... hear, and your soul shall live." The whole curriculum
   compresses to thirst + ear. Pattern: ch. 55's triple "come" (buy, eat, hear) is the
   book's standing invitation.
8. **Look and live (45:22, once — the shortest gospel in Isaiah).** "Look unto me,
   and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth." Five words in Hebrew. Pattern: the
   45-epiphora seal ("none else") makes looking *sufficient* — exclusivity cuts both
   ways: no other savior, hence no other requirement.
9. **Be comforted like a child (66:13) and rest (11:10; 32:17–18; 57:2).** "As one
   whom his mother comforteth, so will I comfort you" — simile detector's tenderest
   hit — ending in glorious rest. Pattern: the book that opened with "children I have
   nourished" (1:2) closes with nursing/rest (66:11–13). Residence, not performance.

### Open veins (next digs, in priority order)
1. **The four Servant Songs as one portrait** (42 → 49 → 50 → 52–53: gentle →
   global → obedient → marred-then-high). Trace *mishpat* + *eved* through all four.
2. **The highway thread** (11:16, 19:23, 35:8, 40:3, 62:10): exodus road → holy road →
   prepared road. Inclusio-candidate across halves.
3. **The light thread** (9:2 → 42:6 → 49:6 → 60:1–3): Galilee spark → Servant light →
   Gentile light → Zion sunrise. Janus-adjacent (or/darkness twin-pair).
4. **The remnant thread** (1:9 → 10:20–22 → 11:11 → 37:31–32 → 46:3): left → return
   (Shear-jashub wordplay!) → root → escapers. Paronomasia detector would serve here.
5. **The leveling series** (24:2 people/priest, buyer/seller, lender/borrower…):
   status unmade before presence — the social side of "come down to come up."
6. **"I am" declarations** (43:11 → 44:6 → 45:5–6,14,18,22 → 46:9 → 48:12): nine
   self-sealings; map against the 45-epiphora for the sovereignty dossier.
7. **Zion's watchmen** (52:7–8 "see eye to eye" + 62:6–7 never-silent): the communal
   end of the path — coming unto God ends in keeping others awake (Ps 134 night
   service, essay §6).
