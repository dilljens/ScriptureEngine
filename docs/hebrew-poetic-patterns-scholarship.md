Online research 2026-10-08. Primary authorities: Lowth → Kugel/Alter → Berlin/Jakobson → Ugaritic word-pair school → Isaiah-structure school (Gileadi, Menorah-pattern, 1QIsa chiasmus studies). This file is the *external* complement to `docs/hebrew-patterns-symbols-catalog.md` (which inventories what is in our DB).

## 1. Parallelism — the foundation (Lowth 1753/1778 → today)

Lowth (*De sacra poesi Hebraeorum*; *Isaiah* commentary) defined it: proposition + subjoined equivalent/contrast, in sense or grammatical form. Three sorts:
- **Synonymous** — same sense, varied expression (Ps 112:1; Ps 103:10 "not by our sins… / not by our transgressions…").
- **Antithetic** — opposition word-to-word or sentiment (Prov 10:1 wise son / foolish son).
- **Synthetic/constructive/formal** — correspondence of shape only (Eccl 11:2 "give to seven, also to eight / for you know not what evil…"). Loosest; much criticized as catch-all; some deny it is parallelism at all.

Correction (Kugel 1981; Alter 1983/85): second line is not mere repetition but **advance** — "A, and what's more, B"; consequentiality/intensification (Ps 18:8 smoke/fire/coals). Both sameness AND difference are present; the tension is the pleasure. Berlin's synthesis: analyze all four linguistic levels at once (below).

## 2. Berlin's four levels (Dynamics of Biblical Parallelism, 1985; Jakobson 1966 program)

Every parallelism may activate equivalences/contrasts on:
1. **Grammatical** — same deep structure, varied surface: nominal//verbal clause (Mic 6:2); positive//negative (Prov 6:20); subject→object swap (Gen 27:29); mood shift indicative//interrogative (Ps 6:5); noun//pronoun (Ps 33:2); noun//verb (Ps 145:18); tense qtl//yqtl (Ps 26:4); stem qal//niphal (Ps 24:7 "lift up / be lifted"); number sing//pl (Prov 14:12 path/paths); definiteness (Lam 3:47). Rule: any grammatically substitutable forms can pair (Kiparsky).
2. **Lexical** — not just *fixed* pairs: any associated words pair by normal paradigmatic (same-class: opposite, superordinate, coordinate — father/mother/son; cat/dog) or syntagmatic (same-sequence: idiom completion, cottage→cheese) rules. Includes split stereotypes ("horses and chariots" → Zech 9:10; Ps 20:7), split names (Balak // king of Moab; Ephrathah // Bethlehem), chair//sit, write//book. Lexical pairing can occur WITHOUT grammatical parallelism (Ps 111:6 people//nations) and can add tension against semantic pairing (Job 5:14 day-noon vs day-night).
3. **Semantic** — paradigmatic (similar meaning) vs syntagmatic (sequence: cause/result, clarify/disambiguate). Often both: Isa 40:9 herald actions sequential, vocatives Zion/Jerusalem paradigmatic. Second line frequently disambiguates the first (Prov 26:9 thorn/proverb).
4. **Phonological** — sound pairs reinforce the bond: 'lwm//'lwh (peace/tranquility, Ps 122:7); bwrk/b'rk (cistern/well, Prov 5:15); šwṭ/šbṭ (whip/rod, Prov 26:3); mw'dym // yd' mbw'w (Ps 104:19); mqrbk // mrkbtyk (Mic 5:9 horses/chariots). Patterned AABB/ABAB/ABBA. More equivalences = stronger unity perception.

## 3. Named subtypes (cut across Lowth's three)

- **Chiastic AB//BA** (Jer 4:5 "proclaim in Judah / in Jerusalem announce"; Ps 91:14; Ps 33:6 with middle term C: word/heavens/made/host/breath). Scales: line → stanza → book (macro-chiasms in catalog A1).
- **Staircase / step / repetitive** — verbatim repeat + addition: Judg 5:12 "awake, awake, Deborah / awake, awake, chant"; Ps 97:5 "mountains melt before the LORD / before the LORD of all earth" (ab||bc); our DB's 186 `staircase_chain`s are this family. Cf. Isa 40–48 refrains, "awake, awake" (51:9, 52:1).
- **Emblematic** — simile/metaphor as one colon: Ps 42:2 "as hind yearns / so my soul."
- **Janus (double-duty)** — one word, two senses facing both ways: Song 2:12 *zamir* (pruning↔song); Gen 49:26 *hwry* (ancestors/mountains). Lost in translation by definition; detection = proposed, always label.
- **Ellipsis (gapping)** — omit verb to be supplied: Ps 96:13 "he will judge… and the peoples [he will judge]"; produces density.
- **Substitution** — drop + replace: Amos 1:2 "the LORD roars from Zion / utters his voice from Jerusalem."
- **Apposition** — second line explains one element: Isa 63:15 "look from heaven / from holy glorious habitation"; Ps 125:1 Mount Zion described by contrast; Isa 28:15 scoffers glossed.
- **Repetition with addition** — Ps 93:3–4 floods x3 then majesty x3.
- **Tercet (3-line)** — 2+1 / 1+2: Ps 91:7 "thousand fall / ten thousand… / it shall not come" (aab = doubled threat sharpens contrast); Ps 86:9 come/bow/glorify (abc = sequence).
- **Sequential devices** — completion ("our feet stand / within your gates," Ps 122:2); action chains (Ps 80:8–9 brought/drove/planted/cleared/rooted/filled); subordinate pairing (cause/purpose/result/condition: Ps 34:17; 66:18; 60:5; Jer 6:27; 66:20; 147:1; 98:1); statement+quotation (Ps 87:5); statement+question (Ps 118:6).
- **Number formula x // x+1** — three//four (Amos 1:3); six//seven (Job 5:19); thousand//ten thousand (Ps 91:7); ×10 factor. Our `ten_xN / seven_xN` connection subtypes are this.
- **Gender-matched / person-shifted pairs; morphological contrasts** — see §2.1 list; standard diagnostic kit.

## 4. Sound & wordplay layer

- **Paronomasia** — sound-similar moral punch, esp. judgment oracles: Isa 10:21 *shear-jashub yashub* (remnant-returns shall return); Mic 1 place-name puns; Gen 2–3 *adam/adamah*.
- **Fixed word pairs (>1,000 catalogued)** — day/night, heaven/earth, horse/chariot, loyalty/truth. Ugaritic discovery + Parry-Lord oral-formula theory drove collection (Dahood, Avishur, Held, Gevirtz, Boling, Cassuto, Culley, Melamed, Watson, Watters, Yoder…). Order, frequency, synonym/antonym/whole-part/abstract-concrete/common-rare relations all studied. Caution (Kugel; Berlin): pairs became an industry detached from the line; oral-composition premise unproved for biblical poetry; still the largest lexical study of ancient texts we have.
- **Anaphora/epiphora** — Ps 29 *qol-YHWH* x7 (ritual crescendo); Isa 40–66 "comfort ye," "awake awake," "I am He."
- **Refrain** — Psa 136 x26; Isa 9:8–10:4 + 5:25 "for all this his anger is not turned away, his hand is stretched out still" (the woe-refrain).
- **Acrostic** — Pss 9–10, 25, 34, 37, 111, 112, 119, 145; Prov 31; Lam 1–4. Rigid/artificial; debated as basis for metrics (Cambridge HTR article). Presence in Lam shows exilic poets used it for grief-memory, not meter proof.
- **Inclusio / envelope** — Ps 8 ("how majestic" both ends); Isa 1:2–31 hear-heavens // …; Isa 40–55 servant frame. Signals unit boundaries; our `structural_formulas` + DSS seams are the detection surface.
- **Rhetorical question & irony** — "Is Saul among prophets?" (1 Sam 10:11); "How she sits alone" (Lam 1:1); Isa 40:12–14 "who measured…?" (Day-One cross-exam).
- **Particles as structuring** — הִנֵּה (behold! disclosure), אַךְ (surely/only, restrictive), כִּי (for/because — causal hinge). Our top collocations ARE these particles + YHWH.

## 5. Large-scale literary architecture (Isaiah debate)

- **Gileadi program** (Harrison/Brownlee lineage): Isaiah in two halves with seven parallel subject categories each; word-count mirror sections; servant-tyrant antithesis; Zion-ideology minis. In-DB as chiasms 4/16–19 + generators. Status: productive, not consensus.
- **Menorah patterns** — e.g. Isa 28–33 and 57:14–62:10 read as 7-lamp structures. Argued, not settled.
- **MT vs 1QIsaᵃ chiasmus** (BYU Studies): Great Scroll's paragraphing/orthography sometimes supports, sometimes shifts, Masoretic chiastic seams — use DSS seams as control, which our DB already stores (20 sections).
- **Ring composition** (Mary Douglas → Leviticus; extended to Joshua, Exodus 1–15, Genesis flood): center = meaning; edges = departure/return. Our chiasms 1–3/5/22–23 are this school.
- **Patchwork vs predetermined** (Kruger framing): minimalist view = Isaiah is redactional fragments with local polish only; maximalist = book-level design. Honest position for our docs: local devices certain, book-level proposed — label confidence, as catalog already does (0.5–0.7 on integral claims, 0.8–0.95 on line-level).

## 6. Ugaritic contrast (why Hebrew sounds terse)

Ugaritic poetry shares the pair-stock and parallel engine but runs longer, more paratactic mythic lines with formulaic repetition; Biblical Hebrew is terser, more elliptical, more morally loaded per colon (Berlin; Watson; Avishur). Practical payoff: when Isaiah sounds "Ugaritic" (council scenes ch. 6/14/24–27/40), expect shared pairs AND tighter Hebrew compression — the compression is where the theology hides.
