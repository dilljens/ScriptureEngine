# The Law as Prophecy: Feasts, Sacrifices, and Sabbaths Foretelling Christ

*The law of Moses held "a shadow of good things to come" (:verse[heb.10.1]), "the example and shadow of heavenly things" (:verse[heb.8.5]), given as "schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ" (:verse[gal.3.24]) — "a type of his coming" with "types, and shadows" shown concerning Him (:verse[alma.25.15]; :verse[mosiah.3.15]). This companion to "Christ in the Old Testament" catalogs how the Law itself prophesies: dated feasts rehearsing His comings, sacrifices enacting His death, Sabbaths promising His rest.*

## The Seven Feasts as Dated Prophecy

**Passover — Nisan 14.** "In the fourteenth day of the first month at even is the LORD's passover" (:verse[lev.23.5]; :verse[exo.12.1-14]). A first-year male lamb without blemish, kept four days, slain at even, blood on lintel and posts, flesh roast in haste — "neither shall ye break a bone thereof" (:verse[exo.12.46]; :verse[num.9.12]). *QUOTED — "Christ our passover is sacrificed for us" (:verse[1cor.5.7]); "A bone of him shall not be broken" (:verse[john.19.36]).* Nothing future remains in the type; its memorial continues transformed in the sacrament (:verse[matt.26.27-28]; :verse[luke.22.19-20]).

**Unleavened Bread — Nisan 15-21.** Seven days, leaven removed, "bread of affliction" eaten in haste (:verse[lev.23.6-8]; :verse[exo.12.15-20]; :verse[deu.16.3]). *ALLUDED — "let us keep the feast, not with old leaven... but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth" (:verse[1cor.5.8]).* Christ the sinless one; His people purge sin after accepting the Lamb.

**Firstfruits — morrow after the Sabbath** (Lev. 23:11 — the Sadducees read "Sabbath" as the weekly Sabbath, fixing Sunday; the Pharisees read it as Nisan 15, fixing Nisan 16; the essay follows the Sunday reading without adjudicating). The waved sheaf consecrating the harvest, none eaten until offered (:verse[lev.23.9-14]). *QUOTED conceptually — "now is Christ risen... the firstfruits of them that slept... Christ the firstfruits; afterward they that are Christ's at his coming" (:verse[1cor.15.20], :verse[1cor.15.23]).* What remains: the general resurrection (:verse[1cor.15.52-54]; :verse[dc88.88.96-98]).

**Weeks/Pentecost — fifty days later.** Two leavened wave loaves, full public gathering (:verse[lev.23.15-21]; :verse[deu.16.9-12]). *QUOTED for the event — the Spirit poured out with tongues of fire (:verse[acts.2.1-4]), "this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel" (:verse[acts.2.16-17]; :verse[joel.2.28-32]).* The two leavened loaves are honestly puzzling: either the church still imperfect yet accepted, or Jew and Gentile in one harvest (:verse[acts.2.5-11]). Timing and outpouring are text; the loaves are inference.

**Trumpets — Tishri 1.** Blasting, rest, holy convocation (:verse[lev.23.23-25]; :verse[num.29.1-6]). *ALLUDED, principally future — gathering "with a great sound of a trumpet" (:verse[matt.24.31]); "at the last trump... the dead shall be raised" (:verse[1cor.15.52]); "the trump of God" (:verse[1thes.4.16]); seven trumpet judgments (Rev. 8-11).* Almost all remains: the awakening blast of return.

**Day of Atonement — Tishri 10.** High priest alone within the veil with blood; two goats, one slain, one bearing iniquities away; the people afflict souls (:verse[lev.23.26-32]; Lev. 16). *EXPOUNDED — "by his own blood he entered in once... having obtained eternal redemption" (:verse[heb.9.11-12]); "by one offering he hath perfected for ever" (:verse[heb.10.14]).* A national layer remains: Israel looking on the pierced One with "a fountain opened... for sin and for uncleanness" (:verse[zech.12.10]; :verse[zech.13.1]).

**Tabernacles — Tishri 15-22.** Booths, rejoicing, eighth-day assembly (:verse[lev.23.33-43]; :verse[neh.8.14-17]). *ALLUDED via feast background — "If any man thirst, let him come unto me" on the great day (:verse[john.7.37-38], feast named :verse[john.7.2]); "I am the light of the world" amid its lights (:verse[john.8.12]).* What remains: all nations keeping Tabernacles in the kingdom (:verse[zech.14.16-19]). (The "four cups" alignment is Mishnah-era custom, not Torah text — tradition, flagged as such.)

Spring fulfilled in order (the day-precision of the spring feasts is the Sunday reading above; Gospel chronology is debated, so "on the days" claims the pattern, not a settled calendar proof; fall stands as His return calendar.

## Sacrifices and Purity as Acted Prediction

**Burnt offering** — male without blemish, wholly consumed, "a sweet savour" (:verse[lev.1.9], :verse[lev.1.13], :verse[lev.1.17]). Total consecration. *QUOTED concept — "an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour" (:verse[eph.5.2]).*

**Peace offering** — God the fat, priest breast and shoulder, offerer eating before the Lord (Lev. 3; :verse[lev.7.11-18]). A fellowship meal attached to atonement — a communion analogy, not Lev 3's own doctrine. *ALLUDED — "we being many are one bread" (:verse[1cor.10.16-18]), which argues Lord's Supper versus idol-feasts rather than expounding Leviticus; the risen Lord's bread and wine "in remembrance of me" (:verse[3ne.18.7], 11).*

**Sin offering** — for unwitting sins, blood applied by status, nearer God for greater guilt (:verse[lev.4.2], :verse[lev.4.13], :verse[lev.4.22], :verse[lev.4.27]). Graded accountability. *ALLUDED — degrees of stripes by knowledge (:verse[luke.12.47-48]); "where there is no law... no punishment," mercies delivering the lawless (:verse[2ne.9.25-26]; :verse[alma.42.17-21]).*

**Trespass offering** — confession, unblemished ram, principal plus a fifth restored (:verse[lev.5.5-6], :verse[lev.5.14-16]; :verse[lev.6.4-7]). Forgiveness funds restitution. *ALLUDED in spirit — Zacchaeus restoring fourfold, salvation coming to his house (:verse[luke.19.8-9]).*

**Red heifer** — spotless, slain without the camp, burned with cedar, hyssop, scarlet; ashes for corpse-defilement; the clean preparer made unclean (:verse[num.19.2-9]). Death cleansing death outside camp. *QUOTED and EXPOUNDED — "how much more shall the blood of Christ... purge your conscience" (:verse[heb.9.13-14]); suffered "without the gate" (:verse[heb.13.12]).*

**Leper's cleansing** — two birds, cedar, scarlet, hyssop; one slain over living water, one freed; washing, shaving, eighth-day lambs with oil on ear, thumb, toe (:verse[lev.14.4-10], :verse[lev.14.14], :verse[lev.14.17]). Death and flight, blood and water, anointed hearing, doing, walking; eighth day as new beginning. *QUOTED procedure — "shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded" (:verse[matt.8.2-4]).*

**Childbirth and circumcision** — eighth-day cutting (:verse[lev.12.2-3]; :verse[gen.17.12]); the poor woman's "two turtles, or two young pigeons" (:verse[lev.12.8]). *QUOTED narrative — Mary's offering (:verse[luke.2.24]); the child circumcised and named JESUS (:verse[luke.2.21]).* Baptism as "circumcision made without hands" (:verse[col.2.11-12]) carries the eighth-day sign forward.

**Issues of blood** — prolonged issue separates, touch transmits (:verse[lev.15.25-27], :verse[lev.15.31]). *Narrative fulfillment, UNQUOTED — twelve years' issue healed by a hem-touch, virtue going out instead of defilement coming in: "daughter... go in peace" (:verse[mark.5.25-34]).*

**Foods** — daily discipline of holiness (:verse[lev.11.44-45]; :verse[deu.14.3-21]). *QUOTED event — "What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common" (:verse[acts.10.11-15]), governing Gentile admission (:verse[acts.10.28], :verse[acts.10.34-35]); foods not defiling the heart (:verse[mark.7.18-19]); ordinances "a shadow of things to come" (:verse[col.2.16-17]).*

**Tassels with blue cord** — fringes "that ye may remember all the commandments" (:verse[num.15.37-41]). *Narrative, exact term UNQUOTED — the bleeding woman touches the kraspedon, the hem-fringe (:verse[matt.9.20]; :verse[matt.14.36]): she grasps remembrance itself.*

**Cities of refuge** — six cities, trial, safety till the high priest dies (:verse[num.35.9-15], :verse[num.35.25-28]; Josh. 20). Freedom through another's death. *ALLUDED — "fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope" (:verse[heb.6.18]).*

Every sacrifice predicts by substitution; every purity law by separation-then-return; every surplus detail — eighth day, outside camp, paired birds — by meaning.

## Sabbaths, Jubilee, and Social Laws

**Weekly Sabbath** — creation's rest (:verse[exo.20.8-11]) and exodus rest (:verse[deu.5.12-15]) in one day. *EXPOUNDED — "there remaineth therefore a rest" (:verse[heb.4.1-11], quoting :verse[psa.95.7-11]); "The sabbath was made for man... the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath" (:verse[mark.2.27-28]); shadow, body Christ's (:verse[col.2.16-17]).*

**Sabbatical year** — land rests, debts released, slaves freed (:verse[exo.23.10-11]; :verse[lev.25.1-7]; :verse[deu.15.1-18]). Bondage temporary under covenant; broken release judged (:verse[neh.5.1-13]; :verse[jer.34.8-22]). *UNQUOTED directly; enacted in history.*

**Jubilee fiftieth** — trumpet on Atonement Day, liberty proclaimed, every man to his possession (:verse[lev.25.8-17]). *QUOTED — "to preach deliverance... to set at liberty... the acceptable year of the Lord" (:verse[luke.4.18-19], stopping before vengeance).* D&C enacts no Jubilee directly — say so.

**Firstborn redemption** — consecrated, redeemed by price or lamb (:verse[exo.13.2], :verse[exo.13.12-15]; :verse[num.3.40-51]; :verse[num.18.15-16]). *QUOTED — "bringeth in the firstbegotten" (:verse[heb.1.6]); ALLUDED — "firstborn of every creature... firstborn from the dead" (:verse[col.1.15-18]); "first begotten of the dead" (:verse[rev.1.5]); "church of the firstborn" (:verse[heb.12.23]).*

**Kinsman-redeemer** — near kin pays, reclaims land, frees brother (:verse[lev.25.23-28], :verse[lev.25.47-55]); Ruth enacts, Jeremiah performs as sign mid-siege (Ruth 3-4; :verse[jer.32.7-9]). Incarnational logic: nearness qualifies. *UNQUOTED as statute; fulfilled in pattern.*

**Levirate** — raising seed to the dead brother (:verse[deu.25.5-10]; Gen. 38; Ruth). *ALLUDED — the Sadducees' seven-brother trap answered from resurrection: God of the living (:verse[matt.22.23-33]).*

**Witnesses** — established by two or three mouths (:verse[deu.17.6]; 19:15). *QUOTED repeatedly — :verse[matt.18.16]; :verse[john.8.17]; :verse[2cor.13.1]; :verse[heb.10.28] — and preserved precisely: "by the words of three... I will establish my word" (:verse[2ne.11.3]); "In the mouth of two or three witnesses" (:verse[dc6.6.28]); "in the mouth of three witnesses" of the plates (:verse[ether.5.4]).*

**Divorce writ** — bill for uncleanness, no return after remarriage (:verse[deu.24.1-4]). *QUOTED in dispute — "from the beginning it was not so," hardness of hearts (:verse[matt.19.3-9]).* Covenant infidelity and restoration run through Hosea 1-3, :verse[jer.3.8], :verse[isa.50.1] (preserved :verse[2ne.7.1]).

**Gleaning** — corners and sheaves left for poor and stranger (:verse[lev.19.9-10]; :verse[deu.24.19-22]); Ruth 2 narrates it. *ALLUDED in setting, QUOTED :verse[hos.6.6] — plucking grain on Sabbath defended as Lord of harvest: "I will have mercy, and not sacrifice" (:verse[matt.12.1-8]).*

**Sabbath healings as acted Jubilee** — grainfields, withered hand (:verse[matt.12.1-13]), Bethesda's thirty-eight years (:verse[john.5.1-18]), born-blind washing sighted (John 9) — seven loosing on rest days, "loosed from thine infirmity" (:verse[luke.13.12]). Bond loosened on rest day: Jubilee in miniature.

Sabbath promises the rest creation began and exodus foreshadowed; Jubilee release by trumpet and return; redemption law nearness; witnesses certainty. Calendar and courtroom testify weekly and fiftieth-yearly — "schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ" (:verse[gal.3.24]).
