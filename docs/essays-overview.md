# Essays Overview: What Each Paper Is About

*Ten papers in `docs/`, rewritten 2026-09-28/29 to a common standard: abstract, introduction with roadmap, explained (never bare) citations, unpacked quotes, stated limits, conclusion, bibliography. Verse references were diff-checked intact across the program per the review pass of 2026-09-29; no `[VERIFY]` scaffolding remains. Start here to find the paper you want.*

## How they read now

Consistent and navigable: every paper opens with an abstract stating thesis, method, and finding, and closes with limits plus works-argued-with. The systematic fault of the old versions — names dropped without explanation, quotes stacked without unpacking — is gone; each citation now carries who, what they argued, and why it matters here. Honest bounds are the house style: inference is labeled as inference (Azazel, Pentecost loaves, qavah-as-braiding, Baker-Ricks dependence), zeroes are reported (marriage in Jeu, Paul-as-witness), and failing controls are named where unrun (Yoma/Tamid, hostile witnesses, A/B outcome data). Two register notes: the ascent cluster (ascending-to-presence, becoming-one, fifteen-day, seven-themes) is devotional-theological by design — formation for abiding, not detached analysis — and becoming-one at 403 lines is a manual more than an article. The temple essay is the most scholarly; the learning review is a design audit in paper form.

### 4. Neighboring Witnesses (`neighboring-witnesses.md`, 73 lines)
Study appendix (not doctrine) surveying ascent-adjacent traditions — Ascension of Isaiah, Sabbath Songs, Odes, 1QS, Hodayot, 11Q13, Wisdom books, Philo — under a three-bucket rule: confirm, illuminate, sectarian (do not import). ### 5. The Law as Prophecy (`law-as-prophecy.md`, 124 lines)
Companion to *Christ in the Old Testament*: catalogs how Torah prophesies — spring feasts as dated first-coming rehearsals (on the Sadducean Sunday reading, Pharisaic alternative noted), sacrifices as enacted death, Sabbaths/Jubilee/social law as promised rest. Strongest transitions in the set; new inferences (Pentecost loaves, lampstand, Azazel) each flagged as inference. Limit owned: calendar precision is tradition-dependent; later custom (four cups) is not Torah.

### 7. Learning Review (`learning-review.md`, 77 lines)
Evidence audit of the Hebrew game's design against learning science: Bjork desirable difficulties, Roediger/Karpicke retrieval, Rohrer/Taylor interleaving, Habgood intrinsic integration, Settles & Meeder scheduling, Mogavi gamification-misuse — each study's finding explained in a sentence, then each design dimension graded. Verdict: strong on retrieval, feedback fixed; ranked gaps retained. Threat model owned: grades rest on inspection plus cited effects, with figures carried from study contexts, not measured in-game (logs exist, A/B results don't).
