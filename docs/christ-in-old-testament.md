# Christ in the Old Testament: A Catalog of Him and His Atonement

*Beginning at Moses and all the prophets, He expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning Himself (:verse[luke.24.27]). This article gathers what those scriptures contain — the passages, what each shows of Christ and His atoning work, and how firmly each is anchored.*

## How to read this catalog

Each entry carries an anchoring status. **QUOTED** means a New Testament writer (or latter-day scripture) cites the passage of Christ directly — the referent is fixed. **ALLUDED** means the New Testament echoes it without formal citation — the referent is shown but not sealed. **UNQUOTED** means no New Testament writer cites it, yet its content — suffering Servant, atoning blood, pardoning God, rising Redeemer — belongs to no one but Him. Luke 24 claims more than the quotations show; silence is "not yet assigned," never "not His."

One more distinction runs through the whole: **Savior-role** texts (atone, ransom, blot sin, mediate, resurrect) stay Christ's in the act itself — servants share only the applying (publish, witness, baptize, intercede). **King-role** texts (throne, rod, nations, reign) can layer across Christ and Davidic servants. Atoning verbs are never transferable; reigning verbs often are.

## Christ in the Torah and the Temple

The books of Moses do not merely anticipate Christ at isolated points; they establish the ritual and narrative grammar by which His Atonement can afterward be understood. Lamb, blood, veil, mercy-seat, mediator — these are Torah words before they are creedal words.

**1. The seed of the woman (:verse[gen.3.15]).** To the serpent the Lord declares, "it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel." A single human offspring suffers a heel-wound while inflicting a mortal head-wound upon the serpent: a suffering yet victorious Redeemer. (*Zera'* is a collective noun — "seed" as offspring-lines; the singular referent comes by later anchoring, not by Hebrew grammar alone.) *ALLUDED — :verse[rom.16.20]; UNQUOTED as formal citation.*

**2. The offering of Isaac (Gen. 22).** Abraham lays "the wood" upon Isaac (:verse[gen.22.6]), who asks "where is the lamb?" — "God will provide himself a lamb" (:verse[gen.22.7-8]). A ram is "offered up... in the stead of his son" (:verse[gen.22.13]), and the place is named Jehovah-jireh, "in the mount of the Lord it shall be seen" (:verse[gen.22.14]) — Moriah, later the temple mount (:verse[2chr.3.1]). Father giving His Only Son, substitution by a provided victim, resurrection hope. *ALLUDED — :verse[heb.11.17-19] ("in a figure"); UNQUOTED as direct type.*

**3. The Passover lamb (Ex. 12; :verse[exo.12.46]).** A lamb "without blemish" (:verse[exo.12.5]), blood on the doorposts — "when I see the blood, I will pass over you" (:verse[exo.12.13]) — eaten in haste, "neither shall ye break a bone thereof" (:verse[exo.12.46]). Deliverance by substitutionary blood. *QUOTED — :verse[1cor.5.7]; :verse[john.19.36].*

**4. Passage through the sea and the song (Ex. 14-15).** Through walled waters into covenant praise: "The Lord is my strength and song, and he is become my salvation" (:verse[exo.15.2]). *ALLUDED — :verse[1cor.10.1-2] (baptized unto Moses); :verse[rev.15.3] (song of Moses and of the Lamb).*

**5. Bread and water in the wilderness (Ex. 16-17; Num. 20).** Manna as "bread from heaven" (:verse[exo.16.15]); water when the rock is smitten (:verse[exo.17.6]). *QUOTED / ALLUDED — :verse[john.6.31], 35 ("I am the bread of life"); :verse[1cor.10.4] ("that Rock was Christ").*

**6. The serpent lifted up (:verse[num.21.8-9]).** The stung live by looking at brass on a pole. Salvation by a lifted, cursed-form object through obedient gaze. *QUOTED — :verse[john.3.14-15].*

**7. Atonement: mercy-seat, two goats, red heifer, refuge (Lev. 16; :verse[exo.25.17-22]; Num. 19; Num. 35).** Yearly blood "within the vail" (:verse[lev.16.15]); one goat slain, one bearing iniquities "unto a land not inhabited" (:verse[lev.16.22]); heifer ashes cleansing defilement (:verse[num.19.9]); the manslayer safe in refuge (:verse[num.35.25-28]). Propitiation, removal, purification, shelter. *QUOTED — :verse[rom.3.25] (propitiation); EXPOUNDED — :verse[heb.9.13-14] (heifer, lesser to greater); ALLUDED — :verse[heb.6.18] (fled for refuge).*

**8. The kinsman-redeemer (Lev. 25; Ruth; :verse[job.19.25]).** Near of kin redeems what his brother sold (:verse[lev.25.25]), as Boaz does (:verse[ruth.4.4-6]); "I know that my redeemer liveth" (:verse[job.19.25]). Redemption requiring nearness of kin and full price — fulfilled when the Redeemer takes flesh to become kin. *UNQUOTED as such.*

**9. Vessels and veil (:verse[exo.26.31-33]; :verse[exo.25.30]; :verse[heb.9.4]).** Manna pot, budded rod, and tables within; veil dividing holy from most holy; showbread as presence-bread; perpetual lampstand light. Christ as true manna, living branch, covenant keeper, presence-bread and light, whose flesh opens what the veil closed. *QUOTED — :verse[heb.10.19-20] (veil, that is, His flesh); ALLUDED — :verse[john.6.35]; 8:12.*

**10. Moses the intercessor (:verse[exo.32.11-14], 30-32; :verse[num.14.19]).** "Blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book" (:verse[exo.32.32]). The servant-mediator who would bear judgment — efficacious only in Christ. *UNQUOTED; servant-intercessor type.*

**11. The prophet like Moses (:verse[deu.18.15-19]).** "A Prophet... like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken" (:verse[deu.18.15]). *QUOTED — :verse[acts.3.22-23]; 7:37.*

The Torah gives the vocabulary — lamb, blood, veil, mediator — that structures Israel's worship. The prophets spend it; the Gospels cash it.

## Christ in the Psalms and Wisdom

No portion of the Old Testament is more fully claimed by the New than the Psalter — language of the suffering Servant and titles of the exalted King, in words the apostles treat as Christ's own. Beside it, Wisdom bears quieter witness to the pre-mortal Christ and living Redeemer.

**Psalm 22** — "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me... they pierced my hands and my feet... they part my garments" (:verse[psa.22.1], 16, 18). *QUOTED — :verse[matt.27.46]; :verse[john.19.24].*

**:verse[psa.69.9], 21** — consuming zeal; gall and vinegar thirst. *QUOTED — :verse[john.2.17]; 19:28-29.*

**:verse[psa.41.9]** — familiar friend's heel lifted. *QUOTED — :verse[john.13.18].*

**:verse[psa.31.5]** — committed spirit. *QUOTED — :verse[luke.23.46].*

**:verse[psa.34.20]** — bones kept unbroken, the Paschal Lamb preserved. *ALLUDED — :verse[john.19.36]; the verse's own context is the righteous sufferer generally (Ps 34), while John's "a bone of him shall not be broken" cites Passover law first of all (:verse[exo.12.46]; :verse[num.9.12]).*

**:verse[psa.109.8]; 69:25** — office and habitation of the betrayer. *QUOTED — :verse[acts.1.20].*

**:verse[psa.2.1-2], 7** — raging nations, begotten Son. *QUOTED — :verse[acts.4.25-26]; :verse[heb.1.5]; 5:5.*

**:verse[psa.45.6-7]** — "Thy throne, O God." *QUOTED — :verse[heb.1.8-9].*

**:verse[psa.110.1], 4** — right hand; priest after Melchizedek. The most cited psalm in the New Testament. *QUOTED — :verse[matt.22.44]; :verse[acts.2.34-35]; :verse[heb.5.6]; 7:17, 21.*

**:verse[psa.16.10]** — no corruption for the Holy One. *QUOTED — :verse[acts.2.25-28]; 13:35.*

**:verse[psa.40.6-8]** — "a body hast thou prepared me... Lo, I come to do thy will." *QUOTED — :verse[heb.10.5-7]*, charter of the once-for-all sacrifice.

**:verse[psa.68.18]** — ascended, led captivity, gave gifts. *QUOTED — :verse[eph.4.8].*

**:verse[psa.118.22]** — rejected stone, head of corner. *QUOTED — :verse[matt.21.42]; :verse[acts.4.11]; :verse[1pet.2.7].*

**:verse[psa.8.4-6]** — dominion restored under man's feet. *QUOTED — :verse[1cor.15.27]; :verse[heb.2.6-8].*

**:verse[psa.32.1-2]** — blessed forgiven, sin covered. The one pardon psalm anchored. *QUOTED — :verse[rom.4.7-8].*

Unquoted but plain — covenant and pardon: **Psalm 72** (king's son, sea-to-sea dominion, poor delivered); **:verse[psa.89.3], 27-29** (covenant, Firstborn highest, throne forever); **:verse[psa.132.11-14]** (fruit of body enthroned; rest forever, dwelling); **:verse[psa.21.4]** (length of days forever — resurrection in a line); **:verse[psa.51.10]** (create clean heart); **:verse[psa.103.3-4], 12** (forgives, heals, redeems; east-from-west removal). A literal, everlasting, embodied kingship through David's line, still awaiting full citation.

Wisdom's witness: **:verse[prov.8.22-31]** (possessed Wisdom, master workman — echoed :verse[john.1.3]; :verse[col.1.16]; :verse[heb.1.2], never quoted); **:verse[job.19.25-27]** (living latter-day embodied Redeemer — never quoted); **:verse[job.33.23-24]** (messenger-interpreter finds ransom — never quoted); **:verse[job.9.33]** (no daysman — answered :verse[1tim.2.5-6] without quotation).

The Psalter is the Gospels' quotation quarry for the Passion and the priesthood — beside a Davidic-covenant wing (72; 89; 132) and Job's Redeemer, plain but as yet unquoted.

## Christ in the Prophets

The prophets see Him before He comes, naming His work with unusual precision — person, ministry, suffering, pardon, return — and the New Testament with the Restoration fixes many, though not all, of these texts to Jesus Christ.

**:verse[isa.7.14]** — virgin/Immanuel. *QUOTED — :verse[matt.1.23]*, with dual-fulfillment layer (a child in Isaiah's day foreshadowing the holy birth).

**:verse[isa.9.1-2]** — Galilean light. *QUOTED — :verse[matt.4.15-16].* The throne-names (9:6-7) echoed :verse[luke.1.32-33], UNQUOTED as such.

**:verse[isa.11.1-10]** — Branch, Spirit, universal rest. *QUOTED — :verse[rom.15.12] (11:10); expounded :verse[dc113.113.1-6]; preserved 2 Ne. 21.*

**:verse[isa.25.6-8]** — feast, death swallowed, tears wiped. *QUOTED — :verse[1cor.15.54]; ALLUDED — :verse[rev.21.4].*

**:verse[isa.40.3]** — prepare the way. *QUOTED in all four Gospels (:verse[matt.3.3]; :verse[mark.1.3]; :verse[luke.3.4]; :verse[john.1.23]) — of John*, marking a forerunner layer.

**:verse[isa.42.1-4]** — bruised reed unbroken. *QUOTED at length — :verse[matt.12.17-21].*

**:verse[isa.49.1-13]** — shaft hidden, light to Gentiles (49:6). *QUOTED — :verse[acts.13.47] reapplies :verse[isa.49.6] to Paul (participation precedent); :verse[2cor.6.2] uses :verse[isa.49.8].* Servant language extends to His servants without leaving Him.

**:verse[isa.50.4-11]** — wakened ear, back to smiters, flint face. *UNQUOTED in the New Testament, yet His by content.*

**:verse[isa.52.13-53]:12** — exalted yet marred (52:13-14), despised, substituted (53:5), justifying many (53:11), seed and intercession (53:10-12). *QUOTED repeatedly — :verse[john.12.38] (53:1); :verse[acts.8.32-35] (53:7-8); :verse[matt.8.17] (53:4); :verse[luke.22.37] (53:12); :verse[1pet.2.22-24] — and expounded whole by Abinadi (Mosiah 14-15) as God Himself come down.*

**:verse[isa.55.1-3], 6-7** — free waters, seek while near, abundant pardon. *UNQUOTED as such.*

**:verse[isa.61.1-3]** — liberty, bound-up hearts. *QUOTED — :verse[luke.4.18-19], stopping mid-verse before vengeance: first coming claimed, second reserved.*

**:verse[isa.63.8-9]** — "He was their Saviour... the angel of his presence saved them." *UNQUOTED — veiled angel-Christology of presence.*

**:verse[jer.23.5-6]; 33:15-16** — Righteous Branch, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS. *UNQUOTED.* **:verse[jer.31.31-34]** — new covenant, sins remembered no more. *QUOTED — :verse[heb.8.8-12].*

**:verse[ezek.34.23-24]; 37:24-25** — David-servant shepherd-king. *UNQUOTED.* **:verse[ezek.36.25-27]** — clean water, new heart and spirit. *ALLUDED — :verse[john.3.5].*

## Reading rule

Never use an unquoted text to displace a quoted one; never let quoted texts close the canon of what may yet be cited. The apostles quoted what their hour required and left the rest — Davidic reign, Daniel's program, Zechariah's fountain, Micah's sea-depth pardon — for latter-day witnesses (Abinadi, Nephi, Moroni, D&C 113) and for readers willing to do what Luke 24 models: begin at Moses, pass through all the prophets, and find Him.
