Canon parallels: Nasadiya ↔ Gen 1:2 + Job 38 agnosticism; Purusha ↔ Enuma
carcass-cosmos inverted (man as source vs image); Matsya ↔ Gen 6-9 (no moral
judgment, no covenant — closest yet most divergent motive); Yama ↔ first-death;
yugas ↔ Hesiod ages + Dan 2 metals (cyclic vs linear); Manu 1.32 half-male-half-
female ↔ Gen 1:27; Chandogya Being-alone ↔ Gen 1 one + John 1.
